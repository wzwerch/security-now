# FBI

# Summary

In this stretch the FBI removed Exchange web shells under court order, ran the ANOM encrypted-phone operation with the AFP, co-authored the GRU brute-force advisory and seized DarkSide bitcoin.

Documents showed plans to use Pegasus in criminal cases before a 2021 decision against; the FBI also joined the iSpoof takedown.

FBI representatives asked for guaranteed access without calling it a backdoor, and the director replied critically to the industry letter.

Used the All Writs Act, postponed and then dropped the case in March 2016, kept its method confidential and offered it to other agencies.

Director James Comey criticised default phone and disk encryption and hinted at legislation.

ShinyHunters breached the FBI jobs portal and leaked personnel data, including China, Russia and HUMINT assignments, after an FBI advisory offended them (#1098).
