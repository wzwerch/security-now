# IdenTrust

# Summary

The purchase required a CA/Browser Forum attestation letter from a lawyer or CPA verified by phone, and GRC freeware is cosigned with it.

IdenTrust's cross-signature gave Let's Encrypt certificates trust from day one while its own root spread into trust stores.
