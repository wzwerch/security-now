# Marcus Hutchins

# Summary

Hutchins halted the WannaCry worm by registering its kill-switch domain, was later arrested in the US over Kronos banking malware written earlier, and pleaded guilty to two of ten counts in April 2019.

Hutchins registered WannaCry's kill-switch domain and was arrested after DEF CON 2017 for earlier malware; in July 2019 he received time served and supervised release.

Registered the WannaCry kill-switch domain, wrote up the BlueKeep exploit, analyzed the first BlueKeep attacks and argued a worm is unnecessary.

Security researcher known as MalwareTech who registered the WannaCry kill-switch domain. Arrested after DEF CON 2017 over alleged Kronos banking trojan work; pleaded not guilty. Security Now followed the case, the defense fund and the debate over code attribution.

Mentioned when explaining why WannaCry still sometimes works: its DNS kill switch only helps where the domain resolves.
