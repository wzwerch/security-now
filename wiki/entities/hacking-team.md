# Hacking Team

# Summary

Seller of the Remote Control System (RCS / Galileo / Da Vinci) to governments, including the FBI and repressive states. The leak exposed customers, weak passwords, a UEFI BIOS rootkit and several zero-days (Flash, Windows font handling, Internet Explorer), plus weaponized antivirus exploits.

After a 400 GB leak exposed customers and exploits, the CEO told clients law enforcement would 'go dark' without its tools. Steve argued court-ordered endpoint malware is preferable to weakened encryption.
