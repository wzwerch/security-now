# NSA

# Summary

Its nine-page guide of February 2023 recommends updates, segmentation, WPA3, disabling remote administration and UPnP, owning your router and weekly reboots; Steve endorsed it almost entirely.

The NSA released Ghidra publicly in 2019 and said it hoped the release would help recruit analysts.

The NSA's Equation Group tools were leaked by the Shadow Brokers. Episodes 612 and 613 debate whether it should have disclosed EternalBlue to Microsoft and discuss the PATCH Act and Vulnerabilities Equities Process.

Appears as developer of leaked tools (EternalBlue, EsteemAudit), as a Security Now listener asking to host the show internally, as publisher of GitHub projects, and as the apparent driver of Intel's High-Assurance Platform flag that disables the Management Engine.

Steve discusses new evidence that the NSA pushed the Extended Random TLS extension to exploit Dual EC; the agency also tracked bitcoin addresses and is believed to have developed EternalBlue.

Discussed for key escrow proposals, bulk phone records and the suspicion it can break common 1024-bit Diffie-Hellman groups.

Episodes 528-541 mention the NSA as the supposed source of the Dual_EC_DRBG constants and in the context of encryption policy after Snowden.

Subject of PRISM coverage.

Throughout 414-427 the NSA's disclosed programs (XKeyscore, Bullrun, SIGINT Enabling, FOXACID, contact-list harvesting) drive the show; Steve infers upstream taps plus pressure for SSL keys and weakened standards.

Dominant topic of late 2013 and early 2014: tapped Google's unencrypted inter-data-centre links (MUSCULAR) with GCHQ, ran the BULLRUN decryption effort, paid RSA to make Dual_EC_DRBG a default, abused ad cookies for tracking, and built the ANT catalogue of targeted implants. Steve separates targeted tradecraft from mass collection.

Subject of Dual EC, Optic Nerve, Huawei intrusion and certificate-authority speculation in these episodes.

Steve notes governments need no cracking since they hold trusted roots; Regin is suspected Western intelligence work; a Utah bill targeted its data center's water.
