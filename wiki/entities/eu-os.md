# EU OS

# Summary

Proposed by Robert Riemann of the European Data Protection Supervisor, aimed at public bodies with a few hundred users, sovereign and non phoning-home, managed with shared tooling via GitLab. Steve expects bottom-up adoption as Windows 10 support ends.
