# Emotet

# Summary

Emotet began as a 2014 banking trojan and became a delivery platform for Trickbot, Qbot and ransomware loaders. A multinational takedown seized its servers and later pushed an uninstall module to infected hosts. Have I Been Pwned loaded 4.3M related addresses.
