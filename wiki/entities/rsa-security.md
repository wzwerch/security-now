# RSA Security

# Summary

Episode 539 recalls the allegation that RSA was paid by the NSA to make the weak generator its default.

Revealed that its BSAFE toolkits defaulted to Dual_EC_DRBG.

Reuters reported RSA took 10 million dollars from the NSA to make Dual_EC_DRBG the default in BSAFE; speakers boycotted its 2014 conference, with Steve showing his old BSAFE copy and release notes.

Its BSAFE products default to Dual EC and implement the NSA-requested Extended Random TLS extension.
