# Dark Caracal

# Summary

A multi-year, multiplatform operation tied to the General Directorate of General Security in Beirut, using phishing, trojanized messenger apps (Pallas), CrossRAT, Bandook and FinFisher, and stealing data from thousands of victims in 21+ countries without zero-days.
