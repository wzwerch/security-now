# Voyager 1

# Summary

Since November 2023 it returned unreadable data; a memory poke produced a dump, NASA found about 3 percent of the Flight Data Subsystem memory corrupted by a failed chip, and in April 2024 engineers relocated and patched code to restore engineering telemetry. Round trips take about 45 hours.

Will cross one light-day from Earth on 18 November 2026 (#1103).

Approaching one light-day of distance from Earth, as a listener's live-tracking page shows (#1104).
