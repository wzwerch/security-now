# Akamai

# Summary

Akamai's CDN was found to be vulnerable to HTTP request smuggling that allowed cache poisoning; the company had no bug bounty. Its Q3 2022 report documented phishing kits and growing DNS over TLS use among its customers.

Akamai reported billions of credential-stuffing attempts in its State of the Internet report.

Akamai found over 4.8 million routers exposing UPnP on the WAN and showed attackers chaining them as proxies.
