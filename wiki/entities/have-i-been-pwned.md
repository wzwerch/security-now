# Have I Been Pwned

# Summary

In April 2021 HIBP made phone numbers searchable because the Facebook data set held far more numbers than email addresses.

Steve registered the grc.com domain for notifications; the first alert came from the Epik breach, showing the service works even for scraped WHOIS addresses.
