# Kryptowire

# Summary

Its report counted over a hundred CVEs in pre-installed apps on new Android phones, most in vendor additions.

DHS-funded research disclosed at DEF CON 2018 on firmware and preinstalled app flaws in 25 Android phones.

Discovered Adups firmware behaviour (SN 587).
