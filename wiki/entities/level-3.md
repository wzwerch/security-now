# Level 3

# Summary

Level 3's Asia-Pacific paths suffered severe packet loss during the leak; GRC's servers sit in a Level 3 data centre.

Shut GRC's connection when a 13 Gb/s flood threatened neighbours, null-routed addresses on request, gave Steve a dedicated port and offered paid mitigation.

Steve hosts GRC there; Level 3 and Verizon argued in blog posts over congested interconnect ports and balanced peering.

Retired GRC's long-used slave nameservers without GRC noticing; DNS lead Margie Campbell restored service (#1089).
