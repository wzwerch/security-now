# Quad9

# Summary

A Leipzig court ordered Quad9 on 1 March 2023 to block piracy-site domains globally. Quad9 geo-blocks in Germany, is appealing, and argues DNS resolvers should not police content and should benefit from Digital Services Act liability exemptions.

Quad9 contested a Sony music injunction, won in Dresden, then faced a new Italian demand and complied with a global block while preparing to fight.

Quad9 is run by IBM Security, Packet Clearing House and the Global Cyber Alliance. It blocks known-malicious domains, validates DNSSEC, stores no personal data and uses anycast with growing points of presence. Steve recommended it and used GRC's DNS Benchmark to evaluate it.

Quad9 appears in listener questions about router DNS settings and in DNS Benchmark comparisons.

Steve added Quad9 to the DNS Benchmark and noted it blocks malicious domains and supports DNS over TLS but not DoH.
