# Frontiers Saga

# Summary

Steve's favourite current sci-fi series; he also recommended Silver Ships, Honor Harrington and Lost Fleet.

Steve returned to the series after finishing the Bobiverse.

Steve read all 15 books of the first series by Ryk Brown and recommended it repeatedly, praising characterization and nonstop action; many listeners and Leo started it.

Steve repeatedly recommends it and its listeners respond enthusiastically.
