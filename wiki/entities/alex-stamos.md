# Alex Stamos

# Summary

Former chief security officer at Yahoo and Facebook, Stamos agreed to advise Zoom in April 2020 and co-wrote its end-to-end encryption design. His tweets explaining Zoom's policy on free users in June 2020 were seen as adding confusion.

Stamos led Yahoo's security team (the Paranoids) and, per Reuters, discovered the email-scanning software that the company had installed under a government order without telling his team. He later moved to Facebook.

Says the AI-driven vulnerability crisis will last as long as it takes to rewrite billions of lines of code (#1102).
