# Broadcom

# Summary

Broadcom reference firmware underlies the Cable Haunt flaw in cable modems, and Broadcom and Cypress WiFi chips contain the Kr00k flaw. Both cases show monoculture risk where one vendor's code spreads across many brands.

Its WiFi firmware heap overflow (Broadpwn) was patched in Android in July and iOS 10.3.3, then demonstrated as a worm-capable exploit.
