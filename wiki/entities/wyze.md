# Wyze

# Summary

Bitdefender found an authentication bypass based on an all-zero nonce, a stack overflow and unauthenticated SD card access in Wyze cameras; first-generation cameras were abandoned unpatched.

Leo noted Wyze cameras use TLS with per-device keys despite their low price.
