# Drobo

# Summary

Steve studied Drobo's BeyondRAID: mixed-size drives, drives tagged so they can be rearranged, automatic restoration of redundancy, and a choice of one- or two-drive redundancy. He uses one at home and tells listeners it is a cloud sitting beside you.
