# Recorded Future

# Summary

Published a report on voice cloning as a service on Telegram and dark web sources.

The Record's reporting on hacktivist alignments in Ukraine and on nuclear-system hackability supplied much of the material in these episodes.

Its 2019 report on the economics of credential stuffing documented automated dark-web shops, checker tools and typical profit margins.

Reported criminal code-signing certificate markets and Chinese CNNVD date manipulation.
