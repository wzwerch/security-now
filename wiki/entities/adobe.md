# Adobe

# Summary

Exposed a prototype database of about 7.5 million Creative Cloud user records in October 2019; also times patches with Patch Tuesday.

Suffered a breach of about 130 million accounts in which passwords were reversibly encrypted in ECB mode with plaintext hints; also shipped emergency Flash fixes for zero-days.

Moved to twice-monthly security updates because of the AI-driven patch volume (#1088).

Adobe's CSO says twice-monthly bulletins are needed to keep pace with frontier-AI bug discovery (#1089).
