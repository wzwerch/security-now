# Amazon Echo

# Summary

A New Mexico report that an Echo called 911 was debunked; earlier Echo models could be rooted through debug pads with physical access.

Covered for a murder-case subpoena (SN 593), accidental voice orders (SN 594) and new wake words and an ad-silencing audio notch (SN 596).
