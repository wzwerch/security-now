# Conti

# Summary

Conti hit Irish health service HSE and others, waits for quiet times before encrypting, and demanded close to $20M. Ireland refused to pay, got a High Court injunction the gang ignored, and received a free decryptor a week later. Recovery was later estimated at about $600M including upgrades.

Conti began scanning and attacking vulnerable VMware vCenter servers on 13 December 2021, four days after Log4Shell disclosure, then moved laterally.

Conti caused over 150 million dollars in ransom payments by January 2022, attacked Costa Rica and Ireland's health service, suffered the ContiLeaks chat leak after backing Russia's invasion, and drew a State Department reward of up to 15 million dollars.

Attacked Costa Rica's government in spring 2022, then shut its leak site on May 19 and later its Tor infrastructure; members dispersed into other gangs. Leaked chats showed research into Intel ME and UEFI implants.

Credited by GuidePoint with pioneering the RaaS model that AI tooling now professionalizes (#1088).
