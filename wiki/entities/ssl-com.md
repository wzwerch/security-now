# SSL.com

# Summary

SSL.com, with roughly 0.05% of websites, agreed to issue the TLS certificates Entrust sells after 31 October 2024. Customers must validate domain control with SSL.com, but an Entrust intermediate remains in the chain so browsers show Entrust.
