# Bybit

# Summary

On 21 February 2025 about 401,000 ETH were stolen from Bybit by attackers who subverted the multi-signature approval flow via the Safe{Wallet} provider. Bybit offered a 10 percent bounty; funds were laundered largely via THORChain and mixers, with only a small portion frozen.
