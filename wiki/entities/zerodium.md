# Zerodium

# Summary

Mentioned as the established competitor to Crowdfense in the market for full-chain mobile zero-days.

The PHP Git server hackers used the string zerodium as a trigger and a joke comment about a 2017 sale; Zerodium's CEO called the culprit a troll.

Bounties on VPN clients (2021) and Outlook, Thunderbird and WordPress (2022) are criticised by Steve because the exploits are used offensively.

In March 2019 Zerodium raised its payout for VMware and Hyper-V guest escapes to $500,000, outbidding Microsoft, while Pwn2Own contestants disclosed similar bugs to vendors instead.

Steve criticizes Zerodium as opaque and notes it confirmed BlueKeep exploitation; Apple's million-dollar bounty was seen as competing with such brokers.

Raised Android zero-click payouts to $2.5 million and added WhatsApp/iMessage bounties (SN 731); its CEO claimed customers had a vBulletin zero-day for years (SN 735).

Raised payouts to $500,000 for secure messaging app exploits and $1.5 million for iOS remote jailbreaks; sells to governments.

Steve predicts AI-cleaned software will erode the zero-day resale market (#1087).
