# Bruce Schneier

# Summary

Schneier blogged that app-based contact tracing suffers from false positives and negatives and cannot replace human tracers. Steve agreed on the limits of voluntary apps.

His congressional testimony on Equifax and his line that attacks only get better are featured.

His March 2016 survey counted 865 encryption products from 55 countries, two thirds foreign, undermining mandates; he later wrote that the FBI's unlock vulnerability should be disclosed to Apple.

Testified to a House committee that IoT makes everything a computer and called for government involvement (SN 587).

Cited on the DHS memo, Lavabit, Dual_EC, and FOXACID; advises trusting the math.

Said goto fail fits his criteria for a good backdoor and rated Heartbleed an 11 out of 10.

Calls AI agents "genies" that fulfil underspecified goals in unwanted ways, argues AI controls are futile and asks why OpenAI is not charged under the CFAA (#1091).
