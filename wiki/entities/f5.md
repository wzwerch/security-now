# F5

# Summary

It issued emergency NGINX updates in June 2026 for two critical flaws; CISA lists seven exploited F5 bugs.

F5 owns Nginx and patched a critical iControl REST authentication bypass in BIG-IP in May 2022, leaving versions 11 and 12 unpatched.

A CVSS 10 flaw in the BIG-IP management interface (TMUI) was patched in July 2020 and exploited within days; thousands of devices were exposed.
