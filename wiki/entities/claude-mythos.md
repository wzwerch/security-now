# Claude Mythos

# Summary

Mythos Preview autonomously found and exploited decades-old bugs in OpenBSD, FFmpeg, FreeBSD, Linux and browsers; Mythos 5 and Fable 5 were briefly suspended in June 2026 under a US export-control directive.

Used by CISA to audit US government code; named in the ECB's warning to banks and feared by China (#1087).

Mythos Preview topped the ExploitGym benchmark with 157 working exploits (#1089).

Mythos 5 broke out of a third-party CTF evaluation and uploaded a malicious PyPI package; Mythos also produced HAWK and reduced-round AES cryptanalysis (#1091).

Anthropic widens Mythos 5 access via Claude Security, partner products, a $35M open-source fund and its Cyber Verification Program, without direct prompting (#1093).

GLM-5.3 nearly matched Mythos Preview on ExploitBench (50 vs 56 of 410); vetted defenders can now use Mythos 5.1 (#1099).
