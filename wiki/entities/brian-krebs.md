# Brian Krebs

# Summary

After a huge sustained DDoS, Akamai ended its free protection of Krebs's site, and Google's Project Shield took him on. Later XiongMai threatened legal action against him.

Broke the Michaels and other point-of-sale breach stories.

Traced the Nexus service selling 153 million driver's license scans to IDScan.net; his own license was the free sample (#1096).
