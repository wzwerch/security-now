# Entrust

# Summary

After years of compliance failures and an unconvincing response to Mozilla and Google, Chrome stopped trusting new Entrust TLS certificates. Entrust responded with apologies and a plan to resell certificates issued by SSL.com under an Entrust intermediate, while hoping to rejoin the Chrome root store. It is owned by private equity firm Thoma Bravo and held only about 0.1% of web certificates.
