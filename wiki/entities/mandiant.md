# Mandiant

# Summary

Mandiant assisted LastPass in investigating the 2022 breaches and forensically imaging devices; it also appears in other incident reports covered on the show.

Mandiant's ten-year study counted 80 exploited zero-days in 2021, more than double the previous record, and tied them to Chinese groups, ransomware actors and commercial spyware customers.

A book claimed Mandiant watched APT1 operators through their webcams; FireEye said the footage was consensual network captures.
