# Moxie Marlinspike

# Summary

His project's rationale, that every network service has vulnerabilities and should be minimally exposed, matched Steve's argument for port knocking.

CloudCracker for PPTP handshakes; Whisper Systems apps.

Launched TextSecure; Steve unsure about server-held prekeys.

Wrote the critique of Telegram's crypto challenge.

His rule of verifying the MAC before decrypting frames the POODLE discussion; TextSecure and RedPhone come from his group.
