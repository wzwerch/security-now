# Equifax

# Summary

CNBC reported in 2019 that the roughly 148 million stolen Equifax records never surfaced for sale, leading Troy Hunt and others to suspect a nation-state collector.

Equifax is one of the three major US credit bureaus. Its 2017 breach, traced to an unpatched Apache Struts flaw, exposed names, SSNs, birth dates and addresses of roughly 143-145 million people. The show covered weak freeze PINs, slow disclosure, the security chief's background and Bruce Schneier's congressional testimony.

Received the UK's maximum 500,000 pound fine in September 2018. Leo notes Congress then made credit freezes free.
