# InSpectre

# Summary

Released January 2018 and updated through release 6 and later CPU-ID display, InSpectre reads CPUID and model-specific registers to say whether hardware and OS mitigations are present and whether a performance cost applies. It runs under WINE and Boot Camp but is not reliable in VMs.
