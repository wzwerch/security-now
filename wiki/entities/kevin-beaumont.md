# Kevin Beaumont

# Summary

Beaumont demonstrated that Recall's database could be stolen without admin rights using an infostealer, urged Microsoft to pull the feature, and wrote an influential analysis of the Snowflake customer breaches and the role of infostealers.

Named the Follina Office zero-day and advised keeping Confluence behind a VPN or authenticating proxy.

Noticed the first BlueKeep attacks via honeypot crashes and tracked the WannaCry persistence story.

Beaumont linked ransomware incidents to unpatched Pulse Secure VPNs and publicised unpatched counts.

His Root Bridge post documented unauthenticated ADB exposed on port 5555.

Confirmed the LiteLLM credential dump and found a big US tech company's "rotated" credentials still worked (#1092).
