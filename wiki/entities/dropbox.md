# Dropbox

# Summary

Attackers captured a developer's GitHub password and hardware-key code; Dropbox rotated credentials the same day and found no customer data accessed. Steve praised the response and urged breach drills.

Steve found Dropbox reliable for syncing working files between two locations, valuing versioning and online-only folders, while noting encryption must be added separately (for example VeraCrypt containers or Boxcryptor).

Pledged no DMCA or legal action for good-faith research and asked for reasonable fix time before publication.

Two-factor bypass via a dot-variant email account.

Said it was not hacked when hundreds of reused credentials appeared on Pastebin, and co-launched Simply Secure with Google.

Nearly 5,000 accounts were breached via a legacy Lenovo ID integration in August 2026 (#1095).
