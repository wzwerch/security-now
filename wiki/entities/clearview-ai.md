# Clearview AI

# Summary

Faced an Illinois settlement, a UK fine of about 7.5M pounds and a 20M euro Greek GDPR fine; used in Ukraine for identifying the dead. Steve sees GDPR reach over a firm with no EU customers as aggressive but expects the firm to lose ground.

Clearview scraped billions of images, sold search to hundreds of agencies and some companies, received cease-and-desist letters, was sued under Illinois BIPA, and had its customer list leaked.

The ACLU's suit alleged Clearview built a database of billions of faceprints without consent and offers a search app to police and private customers.
