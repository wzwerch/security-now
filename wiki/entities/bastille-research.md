# Bastille Research

# Summary

Bastille researchers examined eight brands of non-Bluetooth 2.4 GHz wireless keyboards and found they sent keystrokes with no real encryption, allowing eavesdropping and injection from a distance. Steve advised using wired or Bluetooth keyboards.
