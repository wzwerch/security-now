# Sandworm

# Summary

The SBU believes Sandworm wiped Kyivstar's infrastructure in December 2023 after being inside since at least May.

Used a Windows OLE zero-day in PowerPoint spear-phishing against NATO, Ukraine and European targets, patched October 2014.
