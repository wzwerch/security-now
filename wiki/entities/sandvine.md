# Sandvine

# Summary

Citizen Lab identified a PacketLogic device on the Telecom Egypt/Vodafone Egypt link as the injector.

Citizen Lab reported ISPs in Turkey, Syria and Egypt used PacketLogic devices to redirect software downloads to spyware and inject mining scripts into non-HTTPS pages; Sandvine disputed the report.
