# DeepSeek

# Summary

DeepSeek was banned in Italy, had an exposed ClickHouse database found by Wiz, and its models were readily jailbroken by Unit 42, KELA and others (SN 1011). Berlin later moved to have it removed from app stores (SN 1033).

Released DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731, competitive with proprietary models at a smaller activated parameter count (#1091).

DeepSeek v4.1-flash powered the ARTEX instance used against South Korean banks (#1100).
