# MDASH

# Summary

Credited with record Patch Tuesday counts in 2026; Steve thinks it may beat general-purpose models.

Microsoft runs MDASH at Windows scale with multi-model debate and a separate prove pipeline, and warns of much larger Patch Tuesdays (#1087).

Credited with nearly 1,000 fixes in one month versus Glasswing's 202 in five; Steve sees its model-agnostic harness as the difference (#1096).

Behind October 2026's record 1,411 fixes; Steve expects peak patching soon, then a sharp drop (#1101).
