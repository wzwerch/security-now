# Infineon

# Summary

Infineon's embedded crypto library, used since 2012 in TPMs, smartcards and YubiKey 4 on-chip generation, produced RSA keys that can be factored cheaply. Fallout hit BitLocker, Gemalto cards and national ID cards in Estonia and Spain.
