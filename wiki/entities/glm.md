# GLM

# Summary

Hugging Face used GLM 5.2 on its own servers for attack forensics after hosted frontier models refused (#1089).

GLM-5.3 is the most cyber-capable open-weight model (NIST CAISI); abliterated versions are sold by a startup named Abliteration and hosted on Hugging Face (#1099).

GLM-5.3 supplemented DeepSeek in the ARTEX attacks on South Korean banks (#1100).
