# Wi-Fi Alliance

# Summary

The Wi-Fi Alliance developed WPA3 without public review of the specification, which Steve and the Dragonblood researchers blamed for the flaws found soon after launch. It responded with certification changes and vendor guidance.

Steve criticizes its closed process after WEP, WPA, WPA2 and WPS failures and speculates WPA3 will use Diffie-Hellman-based opportunistic encryption and a 192-bit suite.

Steve criticized its closed process and withheld specification while welcoming the announced WPA3 features.

Steve criticizes its closed standards process; in October 2018 it introduced simple generation names such as WiFi 6 for 802.11ax.
