# Comcast

# Summary

Circulated a lobbying deck against browser DNS-over-HTTPS that Mozilla and Steve rebutted.

Mozilla's Trusted Recursive Resolver program added Comcast, which Steve criticised given the ISP's history of ad injection and lobbying against DoH, though Comcast promised not to track or sell DNS data.

Criticized for enabling public Xfinity hotspots on customer routers without opt-in, and for Netflix throttling and peering disputes.

Injected JavaScript notices into pages on its XFINITY hotspots and was accused of telling customers not to use Tor.
