# APT28

# Summary

Google warned about 14,000 Gmail users targeted in an APT28 phishing campaign in September 2021 and said all attempts were blocked.

Microsoft reported that the group compromised a VoIP phone, printer and video decoder with default or outdated credentials to enter corporate networks.

Named by the FBI as the creator of VPNFilter; Palo Alto noted a shift to broader phishing spray.
