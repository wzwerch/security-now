# Android

# Summary

Android security in 2019 included PNG-rendering and Media Framework flaws, the Android Q privacy changes, and Google's 2018 security report showing very low harmful-app rates on Google Play.

Stories include RAMpage, Kryptowire's 47 firmware flaws, man-in-the-disk, the Fortnite installer, a pre-Pie broadcast leak of MAC and WiFi data, the AT command analysis and CFI in the Android 9 kernel.
