# Tor Project

# Summary

Tor is testing a client-puzzle defense that activates when an onion service is under DoS.

Merged with the Tails project in September 2024; its Firefox-based Tor Browser needed an emergency update for an exploited Firefox bug.

In September 2020 the Tor Project began a membership program for organizations to provide unrestricted funding; a researcher also publicly disclosed Tor fingerprinting weaknesses after feeling ignored.

A researcher published Tor TLS fingerprinting weaknesses in 2020; Tor responded that bridges are a known issue.

SN 514 and 518 examine research (Astoria, HORNET) on improving Tor against network-level traffic correlation.

Carnegie Mellon researchers were reportedly paid by the FBI to deanonymize hidden-service users; Tor Messenger combined Tor with OTR; France floated a Tor ban after the Paris attacks.

Usage rose after PRISM; the NSA reportedly retains encrypted and Tor traffic as suspicious.

Tor Browser's Firefox 17 ESR was exploited via Freedom Hosting; Tor use surged after the leaks and an NRL paper found users more exposed to traffic analysis.

Used by CryptoLocker's decryption service and the Chewbacca POS botnet; Safeplug appliance.

Assessed Heartbleed impact on relays and hidden services.
