# Katie Moussouris

# Summary

Moussouris helped create Microsoft's and the Pentagon's bounty programs. In April 2020 Zoom hired Luta Security to overhaul its disclosure process, which she described as needing internal engineering culture change, not just bounties.

Founder of Microsoft's bounty program and Luta Security; her comments on bounty market flaws prompted Steve's discussion of incentives.
