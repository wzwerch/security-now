# Reaper (IoTroop) botnet

# Summary

Reaper, also called IoTroop, spread through known flaws in routers, cameras and NAS boxes rather than default passwords. Early reports of two million devices were revised; F5 Labs counted about 750,000 compatible devices and noted it had not launched attacks.
