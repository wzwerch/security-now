# eBay

# Summary

A May 2020 report showed eBay pages loading a ThreatMetrix script that checked localhost ports for remote access tools, likely to catch hijacked accounts used for fraud.

SN 457: eBay admitted a breach of names, addresses, birth dates and hashed passwords and cut its maximum password length from 64 to 20.
