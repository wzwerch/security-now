# BlackBerry

# Summary

Court documents in 2016 showed the RCMP had a BlackBerry global decryption key for consumer PIN-to-PIN messages and had decrypted roughly a million messages in one case. BlackBerry's CEO responded with a vague 'lawful access' blog post. Enterprise BES customers used their own keys.
