# DoppelPaymer

# Summary

DoppelPaymer derives from BitPaymer, added multithreaded encryption, and leaks stolen documents to pressure victims. Steve noted its attacks on Compal, Delaware County (paid about 500,000 dollars), Banijay and Foxconn (a roughly 35 million dollar demand).
