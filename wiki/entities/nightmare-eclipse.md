# Nightmare Eclipse

# Summary

Leaks include BlueHammer, RedSun, GreenPlasma, MiniPlasma, YellowKey, UnDefend and RoguePlanet in a dispute with Microsoft's disclosure process.

RoguePlanet was patched on the fly via Defender; the promised "bone-shattering" July exploit may be scaled back, and the earlier BitLocker PIN-bypass claim was knowingly false (#1087).

The promised "bone-crushing" exploit became LegacyHive, a deliberately limited registry-hive elevation of privilege (#1088).

Revealed himself as ex-Microsoft researcher Abdelhamid Naceri, in a legal dispute over his firing; Steve condemns the revenge zero-days (#1097).
