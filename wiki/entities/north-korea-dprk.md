# North Korea (DPRK)

# Summary

Episodes cover DPRK laptop farms (Christina Chapman sentenced to 8.5 years), embedding IT workers inside crypto firms, and a record $2 billion of crypto theft in 2025 representing a large share of the country's hard currency.

The US warned in May 2024 that one network used identities of more than 60 real Americans to obtain remote jobs; KnowBe4's case showed the laptop-farm technique.
