# Hover

# Summary

Clean, no-upsell registrar with free WHOIS privacy.

Steve recommends registering domains in your own registrar account (he uses Hover; its reseller arm Enom hosted a neighbor's domain under a webmaster's account).

Hover did not support DNSSEC or full EDNS at the time and called itself a retail DNS provider.

Offered TOTP as a second factor.

Listeners overwhelmingly recommended it; it includes WHOIS privacy and does not upsell.
