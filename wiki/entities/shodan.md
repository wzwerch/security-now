# Shodan

# Summary

Steve used Shodan repeatedly as the explanation for how exposed debuggers, webcams, cable modems, databases and vendor services are found by researchers and attackers alike. It identified a public Werkzeug debugger at Patreon, hundreds of thousands of Arris modems and exposed MongoDB servers.

Cited as how attackers locate vulnerable devices at scale after a flaw is found.
