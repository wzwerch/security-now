# Avaddon

# Summary

Avaddon ran from 2019 and grew aggressive after June 2020, using RDP and VPN credentials, data theft, leak sites and DDoS. After attacking AXA's Asian units it went private, then shut down and gave BleepingComputer all its keys. AdvIntel estimated about $87M in total ransoms and tied the retreat to state pressure after Colonial Pipeline.
