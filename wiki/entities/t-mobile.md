# T-Mobile

# Summary

A hacker gained access through an exposed router and took identity data on current, former and prospective customers; Steve used it to discuss credit freezes and SIM swap risk.

About 15 million applicants from 2013 to 2015 were affected in the Experian breach discussed in episode 528.
