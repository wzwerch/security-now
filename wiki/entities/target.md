# Target

# Summary

Pranksters repeatedly played explicit audio over store loudspeakers because the PA was an extension reachable from outside lines, an example of exposing an internal system.

Point-of-sale malware (BlackPOS) on XP Embedded terminals exposed about 110 million customers; staged via weak internal passwords and a hijacked FTP server.

Its 2013 point-of-sale breach began with credentials stolen from HVAC contractor Fazio Mechanical.

Its BlackPOS breach is the template for the Home Depot attack a year later on similar XP Embedded terminals.
