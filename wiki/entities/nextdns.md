# NextDNS

# Summary

Founded in 2019, NextDNS joined Cloudflare in Mozilla's Trusted Recursive Resolver program, offering block lists and logs to users who want DNS control.

NextDNS, the secondary provider in Firefox's DoH list, was swamped when Firefox 77 enabled automatic DoH, prompting Mozilla to pause the rollout. Leo also recommended it as a malware and ad-blocking resolver.
