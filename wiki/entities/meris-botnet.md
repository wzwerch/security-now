# Meris botnet

# Summary

Meris, named by Qrator Labs and Yandex, drove HTTPS layer-7 attacks peaking at 21.8 million requests per second in September 2021 from roughly 330,000 hosts, mostly MikroTik routers. Steve uses it to explain the evolution of DoS attacks and why valid, non-spoofed requests are hard to filter.
