# Linux.Wifatch

# Summary

Symantec's 2015 write-up described tens of thousands of infected routers, mostly in China and Brazil, with no malicious payload. The worm killed Telnet and other exposed services and removed other malware; its author said it was altruistic. Steve noted such immunizing worms are illegal but this outcome seemed benign.
