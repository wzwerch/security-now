# Electronic Frontier Foundation

# Summary

The EFF advised pausing PGP plugins during eFail and launched STARTTLS Everywhere with Certbot plugins and a policy preload list.

The EFF filed prior art against a podcasting patent, supports Let's Encrypt, and was sued by a patent attorney over a patent criticism.

Who Has Your Back ratings, data broker FAQ, Oracle v Google brief, bitcoin donations.

Published the Encrypt the Web scorecard and a 3.5 of 12 grade for the President's NSA speech.

Published the secure messaging scorecard and led the Let's Encrypt initiative.
