# DNS4EU

# Summary

Built under ENISA supervision by a consortium led by Whalebone, DNS4EU supports DNSSEC, DoH and DoT and offers protective, child-protection, ad-blocking and unfiltered profiles. Steve measured high latency from California, while EU listeners report good speeds.
