# Minimum Viable Secure Product (MVSP)

# Summary

Contributors include Google, Salesforce, Okta and Slack, and CISA joined in April 2024. The checklist covers disclosure policy, breach notification, HTTPS/HSTS, password rules, logging, patch timelines, access review and backups, and is meant as a procurement tool.
