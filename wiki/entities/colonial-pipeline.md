# Colonial Pipeline

# Summary

Colonial shut down operations after an IT-side ransomware infection, disrupting about 45% of East Coast fuel supply. Mandiant's findings pointed to a dormant VPN account without multifactor authentication whose password appeared in leaked dumps. Colonial paid about $4.4M but restored mostly from backups because the decryptor was slow.
