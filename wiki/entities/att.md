# AT&T

# Summary

A dataset first offered in 2021 became public in 2024 and included SSNs, birth dates and weakly encrypted account passcodes. AT&T moved from denial to admitting 7.6 million current and 65.4 million former holders were in the data, then notified 51.2 million people. It also had a 12-hour nationwide outage in February 2024.

The AT&T data was two years old and had not been deleted from the Snowflake environment, adding to the criticism of data retention.

Criticized by Steve for the U-Verse gateway negligence; also an ISP voice in the Net Neutrality debate.

Also injected a UIDH header, then suspended its test in November 2014.
