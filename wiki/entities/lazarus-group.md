# Lazarus Group

# Summary

Attributed by Arkham, Elliptic and the FBI with the roughly 1.5 billion USD Bybit theft in February 2025, carried out by compromising Safe{Wallet} and altering the multisig wallet UI. Similar AFD.sys privilege-escalation bugs were previously linked to the group.

TRM and Chainalysis attribute the $6.3 billion Binance theft to the North Korean group TraderTraitor (#1103).
