# Lizard Squad

# Summary

Attacked PlayStation Network and sent a bomb-threat tweet grounding a Sony executive's flight.

Used old SYN-flood style attacks and a botnet of hijacked home routers; stopped after Kim Dotcom gave them Mega vouchers.
