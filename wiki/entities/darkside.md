# DarkSide

# Summary

DarkSide launched around August 2020 with Salsa20 plus RSA-1024 encryption, double extortion and a self-styled ethics code. After the Colonial Pipeline outage it said it lost its servers and closed. The DOJ later seized about 63.7 BTC of the ransom, and affiliates reappeared under other names.
