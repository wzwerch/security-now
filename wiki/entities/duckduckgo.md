# DuckDuckGo

# Summary

Handled 34.8 billion queries in 2021 and announced a desktop browser built on operating-system rendering engines rather than a Chromium fork; Steve questioned extension support.

Its browser let Microsoft trackers load because of a search syndication agreement, which it promised to revisit.

Its extension and apps add tracking protection and HTTPS upgrading.

Steve highlighted DuckDuckGo's rapidly growing traffic and its direct result links compared with Google's tracked redirect links.
