# CISA

# Summary

Extended Mitre's CVE funding by 11 months (SN 1022) and published Oracle credential-risk guidance; its current capacity is questioned.

CISA's Cyber Hygiene scanning service gave GRC a free external vulnerability report; CISA also ordered federal agencies to upload Cisco SD-WAN logs and issued SharePoint guidance. ANCHOR is DHS's planned successor to CIPAC.

CISA issued a rare emergency directive on hybrid Exchange configurations in August 2025 and co-published the NSA memory-safe languages guidance. Reports describe cuts of about a third of its staff and a dissolved partnership framework (CIPAC), which commentators say weakens information sharing. The 2015 info-sharing law it relies on was due to lapse on 30 September 2025.

CISA released a recovery script for ESXiArgs, a red-team report showing an undetected intrusion into a critical-infrastructure network, and its KEV list nearly tripled in 2022, mostly from old unpatched bugs.

CISA co-authored the NSA/CISA misconfiguration advisory, responded to the Unitronics PLC attacks, and launched the Secure by Design alert series.

CISA's ransomware vulnerability warning pilot, its secure-by-design advice requiring physical confirmation for risky router changes, its joining the MVSP working group, and its role as the recommended reporting channel in the nginx disclosure case all appear. CERT/CC declined that case.

Issued the known-exploited-vulnerabilities directive in November 2021 and the Log4j emergency directive with a 24 December deadline.

CISA's catalog and its binding patch deadlines for federal agencies feature repeatedly; Steve questions the value of adding very old or obscure entries but supports the mandate. CISA also gained incident-reporting powers in 2022 and added Spring4Shell to the catalog within days.

Maintains the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, co-published the MiCODUS MV720 disclosure with BitSight and urged Confluence admins to patch.

CISA's Attack Surface Evaluation team uses Claude Mythos to audit government code repositories and has found many vulnerabilities (#1087).

Ended six on-site assessment services (including Cyber Resilience Reviews and Ransomware Readiness Assessments) for lack of staff; automated Cyber Hygiene scans continue (#1095).
