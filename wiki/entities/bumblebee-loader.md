# Bumblebee loader

# Summary

Bumblebee replaced loaders such as BazarLoader and IcedID. It arrives in ISO/LNK phishing lures, abuses built-in Windows tools, steals credentials and reaches Active Directory in about a day. Steve used the Cybereason write-up to stress user-click risk and local privilege escalation.
