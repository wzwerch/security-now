# Operation Triangulation

# Summary

A long-running attack chain delivered by iMessage attachment that installs a full APT platform in memory and deletes its traces. Russia's FSB blamed the NSA and Apple, which Apple denied. Steve observes strong iOS security also protects such exploits from discovery.

A chain of four zero-days plus an undocumented hardware feature in Apple A12 to A16 chips, discovered by Kaspersky; attributed to no one publicly.
