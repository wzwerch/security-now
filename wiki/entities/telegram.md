# Telegram

# Summary

Apple's PQ3 scale rated Telegram level 0, which Steve first dismissed and then accepted after listeners explained that default chats use only transport encryption and secret chats are optional, one-to-one and device bound. Spain ordered it blocked, China removed it from its App Store, and it added controls over who can message users.

Telegram's default chats are not end-to-end encrypted; after Pavel Durov's arrest in France in August 2024 it agreed to share phone numbers and IPs with authorities under court order.

Telegram refused to hand encryption keys to Russia's FSB, was ordered blocked, moved addresses into cloud ranges that triggered mass IP blocking, and lost its Russian reach; domain fronting by cloud providers was then shut down.

ISIS used it for announcements and app distribution, and a Danish master's thesis found two practical padding-integrity attacks against its protocol. Steve said it is fine for casual messages but not for real security.

Steve and cryptographers criticized its home-made protocol, SHA-1 usage, non-default end-to-end chats and bounty contest.
