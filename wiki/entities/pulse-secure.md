# Pulse Secure

# Summary

A critical pre-authentication flaw patched in April 2019 stayed unpatched on about 15,000 servers months later. It was used to deploy Sodinokibi ransomware (including at Travelex) and, per the FBI, by nation-state actors against a municipality and a financial research network.
