# ICANN

# Summary

ICANN's 29 July 2025 notice to WebNIC cited ignored DNS-abuse reports, with a 19 August cure deadline and bulk transfer of about 500,000 .com domains if terminated.

RDRS standardizes law-enforcement requests for redacted registration data.

ICANN urged full DNSSEC deployment in February 2019 after DNS hijacking attacks.
