# Guardicore

# Summary

Guardicore Labs registered Autodiscover domains across TLDs and collected 372,072 Windows domain credentials leaked by Outlook and other Exchange clients between April and August 2021.

Guardicore Labs documented a China-linked campaign that brute-forced MS-SQL servers, deployed a miner and a kernel-driver rootkit, and infected more than 50,000 machines.
