# MailStore Home

# Summary

MailStore Home pulls mail from POP3/IMAP and many client formats into an indexed archive for instant search, letting Steve prune his live mailbox without losing history.

Steve's free tool for archiving and indexing a quarter million emails from Eudora and others.
