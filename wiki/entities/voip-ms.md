# VoIP.ms

# Summary

VoIP.ms lost telephony for days from 16 September 2021 under a ransom DDoS that targeted its DNS, web and SIP infrastructure. Steve notes there is no mature DDoS protection for VoIP protocols, and other providers such as Bandwidth.com and UK firms were hit as well.
