# HP

# Summary

A May 2023 automatic firmware update rendered HP OfficeJet Pro 9020e-series printers unusable worldwide, apparently linked to Dynamic Security ink blocking. HP stayed publicly silent for weeks. Steve used the case to argue for fail-safe update design with backup firmware banks and watchdogs.

HP responded quickly to FaxSploit with firmware for all affected devices, and its devices can auto-update. An HP keyboard driver included in October 2018 Windows updates caused boot blue screens.
