# Zero Day Initiative

# Summary

Mentioned for MOVEit research and the Exim disclosure timeline.

ZDI turned 15 in August 2020 with over 25 million dollars paid and 1,500 advisories; it published a Windows privilege escalation after six months of Microsoft inaction.

ZDI buys exploits, protects Trend Micro customers, and discloses to vendors.
