# Exodus Intelligence

# Summary

Shortly after Apple announced a $200,000 bug bounty, Exodus offered up to $500,000 for iOS, Chrome, Edge and Flash vulnerabilities, selling subscriptions of exploit reports to customers. Steve cites it as evidence that bounties cannot outbid exploit brokers.
