# PayPal

# Summary

Announced default-on sharing of shopping data with merchants from 27 November 2024 outside California, North Dakota and Vermont; opt-out under Data & Privacy settings.

Froze ProtonMail's crowdfunding funds (SN 462) and had a two-factor bypass via a URL parameter reported by a 17-year-old (SN 467).
