# Ghidra

# Summary

Ghidra is a disassembler, decompiler and scripting framework developed internally by the NSA for over a decade and released publicly in March 2019, with full source on GitHub shortly after. It supports many processor families and runs on Java; Steve expects it to undercut the expensive IDA Pro. An early flaw exposed a debug port on all interfaces and was fixed.
