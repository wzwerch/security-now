# 3D XPoint

# Summary

3D XPoint stores data as resistance changes at crossing wires; it is non-volatile, byte-addressable, roughly 1000 times faster and more durable than NAND and denser than DRAM. Steve called it a breakthrough, later reporting manufacturing delays.
