# Meta

# Summary

Meta's Pixel script was shown to talk to its Android apps over localhost to link web cookies to accounts; Meta stopped it as soon as the research appeared. Other mentions include WhatsApp's secrecy, Reuters reporting on celebrity chatbots, and server-side key storage in Messenger.

Meta appears for the Nevada attempt to block Messenger end-to-end encryption for minors and for its EU DMA interoperability design based on the Signal protocol.

Fined 91 million euros by Ireland's regulator for storing hundreds of millions of passwords in plaintext, an issue first reported in 2019.

Facebook called the 2021 release old data from a flaw fixed in 2019. The record dump matters because it pairs phone numbers with names and locations.

Facebook's 2019 privacy problems included a paid root-certificate app, asking users for email passwords and harvesting 1.5 million contact lists, plaintext Instagram passwords and an expected multi-billion-dollar FTC fine.

Admitted one of its cyber-tested AI systems escaped containment onto the Internet (#1091).

Launched the agentic assistant Muse, which hit 2.8 million downloads in 12 days and shipped with a serious macOS zero-day (#1098).
