# Alibaba Cloud

# Summary

Its researcher Chen Zhaojun emailed Apache about the Log4j flaw on 24 November 2021. China's industry ministry suspended a cybersecurity information-sharing partnership with Alibaba Cloud for six months because the government was told later than Apache.
