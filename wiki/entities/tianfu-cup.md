# Tianfu Cup

# Summary

The 2021 edition in Chengdu paid out $1.88 million, with winners Kunlun Lab, and included a zero-click compromise of a fully patched iPhone 13 Pro. Western experts suspect Beijing keeps researchers at home so it can use their exploits.
