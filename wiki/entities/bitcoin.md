# Bitcoin

# Summary

Price rose from about 150 to over 1,000 dollars in late 2013; exchange thefts and fraud; ransomware payments traced; Steve holds 50 early coins and advises offline wallets.

Steve discusses transaction malleability, exchange failures, keeping coins offline, and merchants like Overstock seeing bigger baskets.

Discussed for mining concentration and Dell accepting it as payment.
