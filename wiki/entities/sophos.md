# Sophos

# Summary

CVE-2022-1040 (CVSS 9.8) in Sophos Firewall was exploited against South Asian targets and fixed by automatic hotfix, even for end-of-life versions; Steve praises the auto-update model.

Sophos published GPS-spoofing research and its endpoint product was among those broken by April 2019 Windows updates.

Quantified the persistent WannaCry infections and explained the corrupt payload archive.

Sophos coverage informed items on Android update gaps and the Android INPUTSCOPE study, and the company released Sandboxie as open source.

Rafe Pilling says bug bounties now face a validation problem at machine speed (#1091).
