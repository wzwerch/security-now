# GnuPG

# Summary

Leo's PGP tool of choice, covered with the OpenPGP standard in 418.

After years of meager donations and a near shutdown, a ProPublica story led to a funding goal met in one day, with Facebook, Stripe and the Linux Foundation pledging support.
