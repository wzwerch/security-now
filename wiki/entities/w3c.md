# W3C

# Summary

W3C Verifiable Credentials 2.0 and the joint W3C/IAB workshop on age-based restrictions are central to Steve's hopes for privacy-preserving age assertion. The EU and Spanish prototype uses W3C credentials presented via OpenID4VP.
