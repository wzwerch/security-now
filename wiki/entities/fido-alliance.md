# FIDO Alliance

# Summary

Announced the Credential Exchange Protocol (CXP) and the Passkey Central adoption site at its October 2024 conference, with major password managers and platform vendors participating.

In May 2022 the FIDO Alliance with Apple, Google and Microsoft announced support for phone-based and cloud-synced multi-device FIDO credentials; Steve sees it as pragmatic compromise short of SQRL.

Officials said bulk passkey export is a future iteration; a FIDO video in August 2022 showed cross-device sign-in by QR code but no cross-vendor sharing.

Microsoft joined in December 2013; Steve compares Google's token-based U2F unfavourably with SQRL.

Published specs in February 2014; Steve compares U2F (second factor, server stores encrypted keys) and UAF (complex, P-256) with SQRL.

UAF and U2F specs; Steve contrasts them with SQRL and a FIDO architect praised SQRL.
