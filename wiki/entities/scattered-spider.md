# Scattered Spider

# Summary

Linked to the Clorox, Allianz Life and insurance-sector breaches, and later merged in name with LAPSUS$ and ShinyHunters as 'Scattered LAPSUS$ Hunters' (tracked by Mandiant as UNC6040/UNC6395). The group ran vishing and OAuth-app campaigns against Salesforce customers, extorted Qantas, Discord, Grubhub and others, and doxxed hundreds of US officials.

Two UK teenagers were charged over the August 2024 TfL attack that the NCA attributes to Scattered Spider. The group is a component of Scattered Lapsus$ Hunters.
