# VirusTotal

# Summary

Episodes 933, 937-939 discuss using it before opening downloads, hash lookups, false positives and servers that serve it clean files.

Steve routinely scans downloaded drivers and tools with VirusTotal and treats one or two obscure-engine hits as noise. VirusTotal's 2022 'Deception at Scale' report showed that most signed malicious samples carry valid signatures and listed the most spoofed applications.

Steve introduced it in episode 670 via a zero-day PDF upload and recommended it for checking suspicious files and URLs.
