# TP-Link

# Summary

A Pwn2Own TP-Link flaw was patched in March 2023 yet Mirai operators reverse engineered the patch and hijacked unpatched routers. Listeners reported AX21 routers auto-update by default.

Listed among affected NetUSB products.

The R600VPN was named, and a model-specific packet sniffing module showed targeted engineering.
