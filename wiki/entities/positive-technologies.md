# Positive Technologies

# Summary

In March 2020 the company reported a hard-coded ROM vulnerability in Intel's Converged Security and Management Engine, undermining the platform root of trust on roughly five years of Intel chips.

Presented the research at Black Hat Europe in December 2017.
