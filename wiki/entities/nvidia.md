# NVIDIA

# Summary

NVIDIA published a no backdoors, no kill switches, no spyware statement citing the Clipper Chip failure. Its Triton and Container Toolkit were Pwn2Own targets, and China told firms to stop buying its chips.

About 1 TB was stolen, employee credential hashes leaked and expired code-signing certificates were used to sign malware; the extortion demanded open-sourcing drivers.

Agreed to acquire Hugging Face for $12.93 billion (#1095).
