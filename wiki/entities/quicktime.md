# Apple QuickTime for Windows

# Summary

Apple deprecated QuickTime for Windows in April 2016 just as Trend Micro's Zero Day Initiative published two critical heap-corruption flaws that would never be patched. Steve urged listeners to uninstall it, noting some video-editing tools had still depended on it.
