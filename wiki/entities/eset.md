# ESET

# Summary

ESET researchers found that Broadcom and Cypress WiFi chips send buffered data under an all-zero key after disassociation.

ESET analyzed BackSwap, spotted the VirusTotal zero-day PDF and charted EternalBlue usage.

ESET analyzed the Turla Outlook backdoor with PDF command channel, saw PowerPool exploit the ALPC zero day and discovered the LoJax UEFI rootkit.
