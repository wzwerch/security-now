# AdFind

# Summary

AdFind is a command-line freeware utility by Joe Richards that dumps people, computers, subnets and trusts from Active Directory. It appeared in Ryuk and Cicada intrusions as a reconnaissance tool; a listener confirmed its author is a respected AD expert.
