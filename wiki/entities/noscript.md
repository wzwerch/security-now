# NoScript

# Summary

Steve Gibson's long-time script-blocking extension by Giorgio Maone. In 2015 an expired domain on its default trust list was re-registered by a researcher and quickly fixed; Steve cleared his whitelist, then by August 2015 gave up NoScript because modern sites need dozens of third-party scripts.
