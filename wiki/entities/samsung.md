# Samsung

# Summary

Samsung engineers pasted proprietary chip code into ChatGPT, prompting prompt-length limits; Exynos modem flaws and S10 end of life also appeared.

Tel Aviv University researchers extracted hardware-protected keys on about 100 million Galaxy devices because Keymaster let callers choose IVs; Samsung patched in 2021 but older devices remain exposed.

Samsung shipped Galaxy phones with an unremovable keyboard updating over plain HTTP and an early fingerprint design weakness reported at RSA.
