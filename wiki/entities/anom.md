# ANOM

# Summary

Distributed from October 2018 on modified phones, ANOM copied every message to FBI-controlled servers. It reached about 12,000 devices and 27 million messages, leading to more than 800 arrests in June 2021.

Code showed messages were copied to a hidden ghost contact named 'bot'; over 1,000 arrests followed.
