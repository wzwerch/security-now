# Keybase

# Summary

Keybase offered secure messaging and file sharing built on public-key identities and a device chain of trust. Zoom bought it to gain engineers and credibility for its planned end-to-end encryption; the transparency tree in Zoom's design draws on Keybase's work. Leo worried the service would wither.

Leo recommends Keybase for publishing a PGP key tied to social accounts, as a way around PGP key-signing parties.
