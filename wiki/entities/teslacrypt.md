# TeslaCrypt

# Summary

TeslaCrypt used a server-held key pair so victims could not recover files. Version 4.1a added stronger evasion and mass-spam distribution. When the operation wound down in May 2016 its authors published the master key at an ESET researcher's request, enabling decryptors; former distributors moved to CryptXXX.
