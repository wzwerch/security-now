# GCHQ

# Summary

GCHQ officials proposed silently adding a law-enforcement participant to encrypted chats; a 47-signatory open letter opposed it. GCHQ also criticized DNS-over-HTTPS.

Reported tapping fiber cables and sharing data with the NSA.

Partnered with the NSA on the MUSCULAR tap of Google data-centre links and shared cookie-based tracking; Prime Minister Cameron threatened newspapers over Snowden leaks.

Optic Nerve captured Yahoo webcam stills in bulk with NSA help.

GCHQ director Anne Keast-Butler announced a blueprint for agentic, machine-speed national cyber defense (Cyber Shield) (#1087).
