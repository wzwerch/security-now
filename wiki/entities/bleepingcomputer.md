# BleepingComputer

# Summary

BleepingComputer reports on ransomware and breaches and received Avaddon's decryption keys in June 2021. Steve relies on its coverage for several stories in this period, including PrintNightmare patch failures.

Cited for GandCrab key requests, county ransomware outcomes, weekly ransomware roundups and incognito detection follow-ups.

BleepingComputer compiled a list of free remote work offerings in March 2020, documented pktmon tips and traced the ThreatMetrix scanning script to hundreds of sites.

Reported Windows Defender praise, Drupal miners, npm backdoors, the Mandiant hack-back dispute and several malware stories.
