# ShinyHunters

# Summary

The group stole Allianz Life and TransUnion data and hit Salesloft Drift customers by tricking staff into authorising malicious OAuth apps, then threatened Google researchers and posted a farewell note in September 2025. Members describe ShinyHunters and Scattered Spider as the same crew.

ShinyHunters appears in the Gainsight/Salesloft Drift token chain, the Checkout.com refusal-to-pay story, the Figure Technology leak and the Charter Communications vishing breach. Steve notes more victims now refuse ransoms.

Breached the FBI jobs portal and demanded the FBI retract a statement that the group exaggerates (#1098).
