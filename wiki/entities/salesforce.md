# Salesforce

# Summary

In 2025 attackers phished staff into authorizing malicious connected apps and abused stolen OAuth tokens from Salesloft Drift, stealing around a billion records from dozens to hundreds of customers. Salesforce publicly refused to pay extortion demands in October 2025.
