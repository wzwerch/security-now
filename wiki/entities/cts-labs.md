# CTS Labs

# Summary

CTS Labs released a branded site and whitepaper on 13 vulnerabilities (Ryzenfall, Fallout, MasterKey, Chimera) in AMD Ryzen and EPYC platforms. Trail of Bits verified the bugs, but the minimal notice and a CTO letter defending day-zero disclosure drew criticism.
