# VMware

# Summary

CVE-2021-39144 (CVSS 9.8) in VMware Cloud Foundation/NSX-V was fixed a year after disclosure; Steve criticised the delay and noted unpatched vCenter issues.

VMware guest-to-host escapes were demonstrated at Pwn2Own 2019 and targeted by Zerodium's $500,000 offer.
