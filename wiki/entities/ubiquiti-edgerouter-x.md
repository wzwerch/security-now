# Ubiquiti EdgeRouter X

# Summary

Steve highlighted the EdgeRouter X as a small, inexpensive router with five logical interfaces, firewalling, PPTP and IPsec, and about 500 Mbit/s routing capacity, suitable for isolating IoT subnets in place of several consumer routers or a managed switch.

The EdgeRouter X has five separately configurable interfaces, stateful firewall rules, dual-WAN support and OpenVPN (CLI-configured). Steve proposes it as the replacement for the three-dumb-routers isolation scheme, while he himself uses pfSense on Soekris hardware. Listeners shared configuration guides and videos.
