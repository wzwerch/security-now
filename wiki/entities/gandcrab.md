# GandCrab

# Summary

GandCrab let affiliates distribute custom ransomware builds for a share of ransoms. Its operators announced retirement on May 31, 2019, and Bitdefender later obtained its key database and released a free decryptor for older versions.

Used an affiliate and sub-ID model; its code and affiliate base appear to have continued as Sodinokibi.
