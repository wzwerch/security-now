# LinkedIn

# Summary

Its roughly 2.7 MB script probed Chromium browsers for over 6,000 extensions and collected 48 device attributes into an encrypted fingerprint header; LinkedIn disputes the framing.

Its contact-import feature let researchers verify guessed email addresses of celebrities.
