# REvil (Sodinokibi)

# Summary

REvil is the gang and Sodinokibi is its encrypting malware. In 2021 it extorted Apple supplier Quanta, hit JBS, and used the Kaseya VSA zero-day to infect more than a thousand organizations through MSPs. Its malware has a notably sophisticated key hierarchy that lets the operators decrypt any affiliate's victims.

REvil (Sodinokibi) ran a ransomware-as-a-service operation with affiliates, behind the JBS and Kaseya attacks. It vanished in July 2021, reappeared in September, and shut down again in October 2021 after its Tor infrastructure was reportedly taken over. Steve cautions against speculating about why such groups disappear.

In late October 2021 REvil was reportedly hacked again after restoring servers from backups law enforcement controlled; November brought arrests and charges of affiliates.

Linked to the Texas municipal attack and dental-backup provider attack; McAfee analysis shows strong code and affiliate-structure similarity to GandCrab.
