# Privacy Badger

# Summary

Version 1.0 appeared in August 2015: it blocks or neutralizes third-party domains seen tracking across three or more sites and honors sites that publish the EFF Do Not Track policy. Steve noted a Chrome warning that it slows the browser and discussed how it combines with uBlock Origin.
