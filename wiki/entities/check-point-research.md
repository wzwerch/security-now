# Check Point Research

# Summary

Appears in the show for the reverse engineering of APT35's Log4j exploit chain and for finding heap-overflow flaws in MediaTek audio DSP firmware.

Check Point found the reverse-RDP flaws in rdesktop and FreeRDP and the WinRAR ACE vulnerability.

Check Point reported SIGRed, the Zoom vanity URL flaw and the Achilles Snapdragon DSP flaws.

Published the subtitle attack research and the Fireball analysis.

Published the man-in-the-disk Android analysis, WhatsApp group spoofing and the FaxSploit HP printer attack, all covered in August 2018.
