# CCleaner

# Summary

CCleaner, published by Piriform, was distributed with the Floxif backdoor in version 5.33 for about a month, reaching about 2.2 million downloads. A command server delivered a second-stage payload to machines in about 20 targeted company domains, indicating a targeted supply-chain operation.
