# Lavabit

# Summary

Steve examined its design: stored mail encrypted with the user's public key but plaintext when read or sent.

Closed rather than hand over SSL keys; later revealed demand for keys, daily fines and a legal defense fund; offered encrypted-at-rest mail only.

Owner Ladar Levison lost his contempt appeal in April 2014.
