# Marriott International

# Summary

In Senate testimony the CEO described detecting a hand-run database query on September 7, 2018, finding a remote access trojan and Mimikatz, and learning attackers had been present since 2014; 383 million guest records were involved.

The UK ICO announced intent to fine Marriott about 99 million pounds for the Starwood reservation breach affecting 383 million records; Marriott said it would contest.
