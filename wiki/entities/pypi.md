# PyPI

# Summary

Hosted the malicious LiteLLM releases; PyPI quarantined them quickly after about 47,000 downloads in 46 minutes.

PyPI suspended new registrations after a malware wave, announced mandatory 2FA for maintainers by end of 2023 and moved to hashed IPs with a secret salt after a Department of Justice subpoena.
