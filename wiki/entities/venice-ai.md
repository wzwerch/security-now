# Venice.ai

# Summary

Founded by Erik Voorhees, Venice runs open-source models, keeps chat history locally in the browser and does not filter responses. Steve signed up and pointed to it after the OpenAI preservation order made deleted chats recoverable.

Cited as an example of readily available unrestricted AI services (#1092).
