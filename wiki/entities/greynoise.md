# GreyNoise

# Summary

Shows whether a public IP was seen scanning or in botnet activity; NAT means a clean result covers all devices behind the router.

GreyNoise reported mass exploitation of ownCloud within four days of disclosure.

Recorded the first successful SSHole exploitation about 40 hours after the fix, including a Chinese-commented TAC Agent implant (#1104).
