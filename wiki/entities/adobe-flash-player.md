# Adobe Flash Player

# Summary

Adobe shipped a final update with a kill switch and browsers removed Flash in January 2021; Flashpoint and Ruffle preserve content.

A North Korean zero-day via Office documents hit Flash 28.0.0.137; usage fell from 80% to under 8% of daily Chrome users.

Zero-days from APT3 and from the Hacking Team leak, Amazon banning Flash ads, Chrome click-to-play defaults, and multiple emergency updates.

Emergency zero-day updates in December 2013 and February 2014 (integer underflow, exploited in the wild); malicious Flash files embedded in Word attachments.
