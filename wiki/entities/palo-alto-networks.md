# Palo Alto Networks

# Summary

WatchTowr's analysis showed a PHP-based management interface with poor design; Shadowserver counted at least 2,000 compromised firewalls. A PAN-OS exploit attempt also appears in a RansomHub case.

GlobalProtect CVE-2021-3064 allowed unauthenticated RCE via request smuggling.

Unit 42 documented the Struts Convention plugin condition and Xbash.

Shares hit new highs after Black Hat on demand for AI security tools (#1092).

Its Unit 42 documented an AI-agent extortion intrusion that took under 10 hours and used the victim's own AI endpoints (#1101).
