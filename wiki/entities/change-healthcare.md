# Change Healthcare

# Summary

Handles about 15 billion transactions yearly and touches one in three patient records. An ALPHV/BlackCat affiliate claimed Optum paid 350 BTC (around $22 million) and was then cheated by BlackCat's exit scam, while roughly 6 TB of data remained in criminals' hands.
