# Disconnect

# Summary

Co-founder Patrick Jackson was interviewed on SN 525: Disconnect shows live counts of third-party connections, blocks unexpected third-party content while leaving first-party sponsors alone, protects mobile apps via an on-device configuration profile, and works with the EFF on Do Not Track.
