# VxWorks

# Summary

VxWorks runs in routers, firewalls, medical gear, industrial controllers and spacecraft. Armis found eleven flaws (Urgent/11) in its IPnet TCP/IP stack in 2019, six allowing remote code execution and bypassing NAT.
