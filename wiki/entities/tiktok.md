# TikTok

# Summary

Jscrambler found the TikTok pixel hashing emails and phone numbers deterministically and capturing checkout details.

State and federal bans on government devices are discussed repeatedly; Steve views them as partly protectionist given pervasive tracking everywhere, and describes how a DNS blackhole would technically cut TikTok off. A TikTok challenge spread the USB-based Hyundai and Kia theft method.

Bans for officials, investigations and a proposed Know Your App Act put TikTok at the centre of US-China app restrictions.
