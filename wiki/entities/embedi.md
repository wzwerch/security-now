# Embedi

# Summary

Embedi reverse engineered AMT and showed that an empty Digest authentication response was accepted.

Embedi's researcher used fuzzing to discover critical vulnerabilities in a ubiquitous WiFi chipset and reported them in May 2018, demonstrating the attack publicly in November.
