# NSO Group

# Summary

Covered through Project Zero's ForcedEntry analysis, Citizen Lab's 2022 chains, India's plan for replacement spyware and the NCSC report on commercial intrusion tools.

Mandiant named NSO Group and QuaDream as sources of the 2021 zero-days used by commercial spyware customers.

Steve notes Pegasus was used against targets via an unanswered WhatsApp call exploit in 2019.

Recalled for using a WhatsApp call flaw to install Pegasus spyware on about 1,400 devices.

Steve expects AI-driven bug elimination to undermine exploit vendors such as NSO Group (#1087).
