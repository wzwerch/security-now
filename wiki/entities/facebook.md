# Facebook

# Summary

Open-sourced the Fizz TLS 1.3 library, then in September 2018 disclosed a token theft affecting about 30 million users, with Login with Facebook amplifying the blast radius; also participates in the Data Transfer Project.

In December 2018 Facebook disclosed a 12-day photo API bug potentially exposing photos of 6.8 million users to apps; Steve praised its internal discovery. SN 698 pointed to the ad preferences page.

Uses data brokers for ad targeting and was reported using 1024-bit RSA keys.

Bought WhatsApp and Oculus in early 2014.
