# Microsoft Office Connected Experiences

# Summary

Steve urged switching them off in SN 1002; Microsoft then told BleepingComputer it does not use Microsoft 365 customer data to train LLMs and that the setting powers co-authoring, Editor and Designer with simpler ML, though some per-account data is retained.

Episodes in 2022 cover the Follina ms-msdt handler zero-day, the search-ms handler, and the on-again off-again default block of internet-sourced VBA macros that ended up in force in July 2022.
