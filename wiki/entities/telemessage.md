# TeleMessage

# Summary

Its TM SGNL clone forwarded plaintext copies to an archive that a hacker breached in 15 to 20 minutes (SN 1024).

TeleMessage sold modified messaging apps for government records retention. Researchers showed its Signal clone emailed plaintext logs, several hackers claimed breaches, and US departments banned it. Steve used the case to define true end-to-end encryption and to propose a compliant archiving design.
