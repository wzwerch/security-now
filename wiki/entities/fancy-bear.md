# Fancy Bear (GRU Unit 26165)

# Summary

A July 2021 joint advisory described this unit using a Kubernetes cluster for distributed, anonymized brute-force attempts against hundreds of targets, followed by exploitation of Exchange flaws. Mitigations center on MFA and blocking anonymizer sources.

Kaspersky tied exploits sold by Volodya to Fancy Bear in 2016.

Linked to 2016 election hacking in the DOJ indictment, fake Microsoft and Senate domains seized by Microsoft, and the LoJax UEFI rootkit. Steve proposes standardizing on the name Fancy Bear.

Tracked since 2007 and linked to the DNC hack in SN 594.
