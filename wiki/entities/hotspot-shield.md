# Hotspot Shield

# Summary

A CDT/Carnegie Mellon complaint to the FTC alleged logging, tracking libraries, iframe ad injection and redirects contrary to its privacy claims.

Its Chrome extension had a hijackable proxy parameter, DNS leaks and an overly broad whitelist; it fixed them promptly unlike PureVPN and ZenMate.
