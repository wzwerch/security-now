# Troy Hunt

# Summary

Analysed the 73-million-record AT&T dataset and confirmed it contained 49 million unique emails and 44 million SSNs.

Hunt added phone-number search to Have I Been Pwned so people could check the leaked 533 million Facebook records.

Launched Pwned Passwords v2 with 501 million unique passwords; Cloudflare Workers integrate it.
