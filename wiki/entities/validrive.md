# ValiDrive

# Summary

ValiDrive spot-tests 1152 4K regions; SN 1010 explains why a 32GB stick took about 22 minutes (NAND read-to-write switching). ValiDrive 2 is planned as a test bed for the Beyond Recall wiping tool.

Created in August 2023 after fake flash drives were found; episodes 938-942 follow its testing and delayed release.

ValiDrive is a tiny Windows utility from GRC that tests whether a USB stick really holds the capacity it claims. It was downloaded tens of thousands of times within weeks of release and exposed widespread counterfeit drives sold online.

Steve plans ValiDrive improvements as his next coding project (#1103).
