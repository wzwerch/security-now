# Apache Software Foundation

# Summary

The foundation maintains Log4j and Struts, Solr, Kafka and similar projects. In December 2021 it shipped five Log4j releases in a month after Alibaba's report of Log4Shell; Steve and Leo stress that volunteers carry much of the maintenance burden.
