# Perplexity

# Summary

Cloudflare reported undeclared crawlers, rotating IPs and a Chrome-impersonating user agent; Perplexity says the requests were user-driven and misattributed from Browserbase. Leo defended user-initiated fetches while Steve held that a no-crawl wish should be honoured. The dispute fed discussion of AI answers undermining ad-funded sites.

Not among the companies committing to the new robots.txt AI-training token; previously caught stealth crawling (#1103).
