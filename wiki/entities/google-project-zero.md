# Google Project Zero

# Summary

Published the deep analysis of five iOS exploit chains (SN 730) and the CTF protocol research (SN 728); Apple disputed some framing (SN 731).

Project Zero publicly discloses vulnerabilities 90 days after reporting. During these episodes its deadline expired on unpatched Windows graphics and Edge flaws (SN 600 and 601), and the team found Cloudbleed, NVIDIA driver bugs, a WiFi chip flaw patched in iOS 10.3.1, LastPass bugs and the Microsoft malware engine flaw.

Covered for the DOM fuzzer results, Ian Beer's iOS kernel work, the Keeper bug and the WPAD/JScript exploit.
