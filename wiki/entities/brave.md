# Brave

# Summary

Brave randomizes its browser fingerprint, ships vertical tabs and (from version 1.81) disables Microsoft Recall in all tabs by default. Steve Gibson switched to it in mid-2025 after Firefox still showed a unique fingerprint; Brave passed 100 million monthly users in September 2025, though Steve rejects its 'three times faster' marketing claim.

Brave blocked the Meta/Yandex localhost tracking, was tried by listeners after earlier shows, and was dropped by Steve when it ended Windows 7 support. Brave's BAT micro-payment ideas came up in listener feedback.

Brave's privacy team introduced Request Off the Record (Request-OTR), letting sensitive sites ask the browser to forget a visit, aimed at victims of intimate partner violence. Steve hopes it becomes a standard.

Mentioned for layoffs and its own search index and Leo AI assistant.

Monthly users grew from 24.1 to 50.2 million during 2021.

Mentioned as stripping tracking parameters from URLs more aggressively than Firefox.

Brave briefly appended an affiliate code to a typed Binance address, then removed it after criticism.
