# Microsoft Active Protections Program

# Summary

MAPP has existed since 2008 and gives vendors from 24 hours to about two weeks of notice. A Natto Thoughts analysis found 19 of 104 members are Chinese firms, some also obliged to report vulnerabilities to a state database. Steve concluded Microsoft should reconsider including Chinese vendors.
