# CryptoDrop

# Summary

Researchers from the University of Florida and Villanova built CryptoDrop to watch for file-type changes, drops in similarity-hash match and rising Shannon entropy. It detected all 492 distinct ransomware samples tested, often with few or no files lost, with 7-Zip as the only false positive among 30 apps.
