# Amazon Sidewalk

# Summary

Sidewalk lets Echo and Ring gateways relay small messages for nearby endpoints using layered encryption, rotating IDs every 15 minutes, an 80 kbps cap and 500 MB monthly limit. Steve judged the design sound despite alarmist coverage of its default opt-in.
