# Bitdefender

# Summary

Bitdefender waited three years and followed a 60-day grace period after the final fix before publishing its Wyze research.

Bitdefender hacked GandCrab's servers and published a free removal tool covering versions 1, 4 and 5.

Bitdefender Labs reported the Hide and Seek botnet and the Zacinlo adware rootkit.

Disabled its anti-exploit hook in Chrome 66+ rather than risk users removing its product.

Reported that the China-nexus group SilkParasite uses AI to build seven RAT families and rotate indicators of compromise (#1095).
