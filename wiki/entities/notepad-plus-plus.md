# Notepad++

# Summary

Version 8.8.3 shipped signed by a self-created root CA, which Steve criticised (SN 1034).

Targeted attacks over about six months in 2025; fixed in 8.9.1.

Version 8.9.7 listed five vulnerability fixes, likely AI-found (#1091).
