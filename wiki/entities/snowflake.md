# Snowflake

# Summary

Roughly 350 Snowflake customers, including Ticketmaster and AT&T, lost data when attackers used stolen single-factor credentials, many from infostealers. Snowflake blamed missing MFA and pursued takedowns of a researcher's write-up. The name derives from the snowflake schema.
