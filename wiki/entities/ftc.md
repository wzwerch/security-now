# Federal Trade Commission

# Summary

On 4 January 2022 the FTC said failing to remediate Log4j could lead to enforcement under the FTC Act and Gramm-Leach-Bliley, citing the Equifax settlement, while acknowledging volunteer open-source maintainers' limits.

In this batch: the Chegg settlement and the multi-state letter on commercial surveillance rulemaking.

Steve sees the FTC suing D-Link (SN 594) and settling with Vizio over TV viewing data (SN 598) as the start of accountability for insecure or invasive connected devices.
