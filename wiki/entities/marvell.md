# Marvell

# Summary

Embedi's fuzzing found four bugs in the Marvell Avastar 88W8897 WiFi chip used in consoles, laptops and Chromebooks, two of them giving over-the-air code execution without credentials. Steve doubted billions of devices could be patched.
