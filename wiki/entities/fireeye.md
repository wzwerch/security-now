# FireEye

# Summary

FireEye, led by CEO Kevin Mandia, announced on December 8, 2020 that a state-sponsored actor had stolen its Red Team tools, then published findings tying the intrusion to trojanized SolarWinds Orion updates (tracked as UNC2452). It released 300+ countermeasures and later worked with Microsoft and GoDaddy on a Sunburst kill switch.

FireEye denied hacking back at China.

FireEye described attacks on fingerprint data handling in older Android, including on Samsung S5.

Found the APT3 Flash zero-day, a Hacking Team Flash zero-day, SYNful Knock, Android fingerprint sensor weaknesses and the Forbes malvertising.

Demonstrated iOS background keystroke monitoring, found the IE VML zero-day, and showed Android launcher-icon hijacking.

With Fox-IT recovered CryptoLocker keys and launched decryptcryptolocker.com.
