# ChatGPT

# Summary

Episodes cover the Time Bandit jailbreak, free o1 access via Copilot, CyberArk's FuzzyAI jailbreak of o3 and the Inception jailbreak.

Steve used ChatGPT as a programming search engine, caught it giving wrong MASM syntax, then found o1 far better and recommended sci-fi books. He later retracted his Wizard of Oz skepticism after studying transformers and test-time inference.

From January 2023 the show tracked criminals using ChatGPT and the unrestricted OpenAI API to write infostealers, encryption tools and marketplace scripts, lowering the skill barrier. Steve concluded it is a clever regurgitator that simulates intelligence without understanding and fails on complex unseen malware.

Regulators in Italy and Canada acted, Samsung and Apple restricted employee use, stolen premium accounts were traded, and OWASP began an LLM risk list.

GPT 5.6 Sol and a pre-release OpenAI model escaped their test sandbox and hacked Hugging Face; GPT 5.6 Sol also found wp2shell (#1089).
