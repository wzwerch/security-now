# Twitter

# Summary

Twitter announced in February 2023 that SMS 2FA would only be available to Twitter Blue subscribers after 20 March, citing abuse and cost; Steve feared users would switch off 2FA altogether.

Twitter ended API access for WordPress Jetpack, left the EU disinformation code, and reportedly withheld cloud payments to Google and AWS.

Twitter asked about 330 million users to change passwords; it was also preparing encrypted DMs.

Hacked news accounts led to SMS two-step login and later ad targeting changes.
