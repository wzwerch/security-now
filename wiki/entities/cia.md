# CIA

# Summary

The Vault 7 release showed CIA tools using known techniques and stockpiled zero-days. Symantec tied 40 earlier attacks to the tools, and Steve used the leak to argue agencies cannot safeguard a master encryption key.

Vault 7 documents covered CherryBlossom router firmware, OutlawCountry, Achilles, SeaPea, Aeris, Dumbo, ExpressLane and AngelFire. Steve treats most as workmanlike targeted tools rather than mass exploits.
