# Blue Coat Systems

# Summary

Blue Coat received an intermediate CA certificate from Symantec, raising fears of seamless interception without installing a custom root. Symantec then bought the company for $4.65 billion. Steve argued that this shows how easily any trusted CA can enable interception.
