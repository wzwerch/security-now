# Yahoo

# Summary

Yahoo issued state-actor warnings to a DNC consultant in 2016 and later disclosed a breach of about 500 million accounts that it had known about for years. Reuters reported that it scanned all incoming mail for US intelligence in 2015, and it then disabled new mail forwarding, which Steve condemned. Steve contrasts its disclosure behaviour with LastPass.

SN 591 covers the disclosure of a 2013 breach of over a billion accounts with unencrypted security questions; SN 590 covers a mail XSS bug bounty; SN 596 covers the SEC inquiry and an unpassed breach-notification bill.

Sued over FISA gag, planned default HTTPS Jan 2014, and began recycling dormant addresses.

Its webcam chats were captured by GCHQ's Optic Nerve.

Clarified that servers hit in the Shellshock-era attack were compromised through a different log-parsing bug, and announced email encryption work with Google.
