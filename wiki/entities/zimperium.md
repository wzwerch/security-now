# Zimperium

# Summary

Zimperium's zLabs researched and reported the GriftHorse Android trojan campaign to Google.

Zimperium researchers found the Xiaomi M365 scooter flaw.

Released a Stagefright tester that Steve judged the best, expanded to seven checks after a flawed patch.

Episode 528 referenced Zimperium's StageFright detector as the app Steve preferred, though it only covered the original flaws.

Reported the AirDroid weaknesses after months of vendor delay (SN 589).
