# FinFisher

# Summary

German authorities investigated FinFisher for exporting FinSpy to Turkey without a permit; the company closed after raids and seized accounts.

A FinFisher variant appeared in Dark Caracal and, per Citizen Lab, was served to targeted users in Turkey via ISP-level redirection.
