# VeriSign

# Summary

Under a court order VeriSign transferred control of the ToKnowAll domain used by VPNFilter.

VeriSign's free public DNS servers promised stability, security and privacy, but Steve noted DNS queries remain unencrypted. Steve also mentioned VeriSign as his former certificate vendor.
