# John McAfee

# Summary

McAfee launched the first commercial antivirus software in 1987 and later lived a controversial life. He was found dead in Barcelona as extradition to the US on tax charges was approved. Steve recalls a single phone call when McAfee sought virus samples after reading Steve's InfoWorld columns.

Briefly named an adviser on HealthCare.gov.
