# Netflix

# Summary

Found eight HTTP/2 resource-exhaustion flaws in third-party implementations.

Announced a Bugcrowd-run public bounty paying up to $15,000.

Accounted for about a third of evening US downstream traffic in 2014; peering fights with Comcast and Verizon over its Cogent connectivity and its free Open Connect caches.

Open Connect boxes, in-app congestion notices, direct connections with Comcast and Verizon.
