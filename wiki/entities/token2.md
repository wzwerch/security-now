# Token2

# Summary

Token2 keys hold up to 100 to 300 resident Passkeys, include TOTP and offer USB-A, USB-C and NFC, which makes them an alternative to YubiKeys' 25 slots. Listener reports found them workable but requiring six-digit PINs and bulkier.
