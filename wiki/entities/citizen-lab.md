# Citizen Lab

# Summary

Forensically confirmed Paragon Graphite infections (SN 1033).

Citizen Lab supplied the samples behind ForcedEntry analysis and documented NSO's 2022 chains (LatentImage, FindMyPwn, PwnYourHome).

Episode 941: with Google TAG it traced Predator spyware delivered through an HTTP-injecting middlebox in Egypt.

Citizen Lab reverse engineered the mandatory Beijing 2022 app MY2022, found missing TLS certificate validation and an unused censorship keyword list, and went public after the vendor ignored its disclosure.

Citizen Lab fingerprinted the PacketLogic devices used for redirection and was threatened by Sandvine over a borrowed device.
