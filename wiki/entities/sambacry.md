# SambaCry

# Summary

Linux/NAS analogue of WannaCry disclosed in May 2017, affecting Samba 3.5.0 onward; fixed in 4.4.14, 4.5.10 and 4.6.4 with an nt pipe support workaround. NAS vendors were expected to patch slowly.
