# HackerOne

# Summary

HackerOne supplied nearly 300 vetted researchers for the defense industrial base pilot that found 401 actionable vulnerabilities.

An employee used internal access to duplicate customers' reports under a sockpuppet account and was identified within 24 hours from logs.

HackerOne passed $45 million in total payouts and produced the first million-dollar bounty hunter, Santiago Lopez, in early 2019.

HackerOne hosts bounty programs for Verizon, Uber, PayPal and others. Steve toured its public Hactivity feed on SN 720 and contrasted it with opaque exploit buyers like Zerodium.

Reported six bounty millionaires and partnered with Google on a data protection reward program.

HackerOne's 2020 top-10 public programs (Verizon Media, PayPal, Uber, Intel) and Sony's new PlayStation program were covered.

Hosts the Department of Defense's 2018 bounty and programs from many companies.

Its data showed 5% of hunters find 23% of flaws, cited when discussing concentration in the bounty market.

Channel through which Assetnote reported the wp2shell WordPress core RCE (#1088).
