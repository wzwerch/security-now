# Ryuk

# Summary

Ryuk typically arrives via Emotet and TrickBot after a phishing email and also encrypts backups. In 2019 it hit several Florida and Georgia governments and Louisiana school districts, with insurers paying six-figure ransoms. Steve notes it is not sold in underground forums and that the name comes from the manga Death Note.

Behind attacks on school districts, New Bedford and the DCH hospital system; demands ranged up to millions of dollars.

Ryuk struck Universal Health Services in 2020, K12 Inc. and a case study in SN 789 where a phishing click led to BazarLoader, Cobalt Strike, Zerologon and encryption of every machine in about five hours. The gang averaged about 20 attacks a week with an average payment near 48 bitcoin.
