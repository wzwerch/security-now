# Zyxel

# Summary

Mirai variants exploited unpatched CPE routers (SN 1011).

The zyfwp account affected over 100,000 enterprise devices and was scanned for within days of disclosure.

Nearly 100,000 ZyXEL devices, mostly in Argentina, were taken over by a Mirai variant in late 2017.
