# Pwn2Own

# Summary

Pwn2Own Berlin 2025 paid 1,078,750 dollars for 28 unique zero-days against fully patched targets, with STAR Labs SG as Master of Pwn and seven bugs from the new AI category.

In 2024 Manfred Paul exploited Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, 29 zero-days were shown for $1.1 million, and Mozilla patched Firefox within about 21 hours.

The Austin 2021 edition produced 61 new bugs and over 1 million dollars in prizes; the NetUSB bug was found while preparing for the 2021 Mobile contest.

Pwn2Own Vancouver 2019 paid $545,000 for 19 bugs, including a Tesla Model 3 browser hack and VMware escapes by Fluoroacetate.

Contestants submitted exploits in advance and Team Fluoroacetate won again, with successes against Safari on macOS, Windows, Ubuntu, Adobe Reader and VirtualBox; only a VMware Workstation attempt failed.

The 2018 contest in Vancouver had no Chinese teams; only about $267,000 of $2 million was awarded, Richard Zhu was Master of Pwn, and Safari, Edge, Firefox and VirtualBox fell.
