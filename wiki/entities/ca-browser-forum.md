# CA/Browser Forum

# Summary

Its ballots shortened TLS certificate lifetimes toward 47 days by 2029 and cut code-signing certificate lifetimes from 39 to 15 months from March 2026. Steve notes all yes votes on the code-signing ballot came from certificate issuers.

The forum's baseline requirements (such as 120-hour revocation of mis-issued certificates and upcoming mandatory pre-issuance linting, ballot SC-75) are voted on by CAs themselves, which Steve describes as a model of self-governance.

Made OCSP optional (ballot SC-063) and is considering Apple's proposal to cut maximum certificate lifetimes to 45 days.
