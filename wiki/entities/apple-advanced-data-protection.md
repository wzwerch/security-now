# Apple Advanced Data Protection

# Summary

The UK demanded worldwide access to ADP data under the Investigatory Powers Act. Apple stopped offering ADP to new UK users on 21 February 2025 and appealed to the Investigatory Powers Tribunal. Without ADP Apple already holds iCloud keys.

Announced December 2022 and made global with iOS 16.3 in January 2023. It covers backups, Drive, Photos, Notes and more but requires all signed-in devices to run supporting OS versions, which Steve found a high bar. iOS 16.3 also added FIDO security key support for Apple ID.
