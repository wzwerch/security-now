# Do Not Track

# Summary

A Berlin court ruled LinkedIn must honour DNT; GPC is the successor signal.

Proposed 2009, undermined by the Internet Explorer default and abandoned by W3C in 2019.

Microsoft stopped enabling it by default to meet the W3C user-choice requirement.

EFF, Disconnect and others published a DNT policy in 2015; Steve hopes enforceable DNT could coexist with ad counting.
