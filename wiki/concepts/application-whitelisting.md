# Application whitelisting

# Summary

Good but not a substitute for patching.

Steve and listeners champion Windows AppLocker, including a university IT director reporting zero infections in four years across 400 machines, as the real fix after Sony; works with publisher-signature rules and local policy on Ultimate or Enterprise.
