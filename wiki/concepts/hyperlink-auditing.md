# Hyperlink auditing (link ping)

# Summary

The ping attribute sends an asynchronous POST to listed URLs without same-origin limits. Chrome made it unavoidable, Mozilla planned to enable it by default, Brave and uBlock Origin block it, and attackers abused it for a DDoS.
