# Patch Tuesday

# Summary

Covered monthly with counts of fixes and exploited zero-days; January 2025 was a record at 159 CVEs and eight zero-days. Steve's recurring advice: do not expose legacy Windows services directly.

April 2023 fixed 97 flaws, June 73 with 26 RCEs.

November 2021 had 55 CVEs; January 2022 had 97, with server updates pulled for boot loops.

Episodes review May (domain controller breakage), July (84 flaws) and August 2022 (121 CVEs, DogWalk).

February to April 2019 releases were large, fixed win32k zero-days and caused AV conflicts.

Counts were 115, 113 and 111 fixes in March, April and May 2020, including zero-days in the Adobe Type library and the SMBGhost saga.

Steve's typical advice is to update soon; the December 2015 bundle had eight critical items and coincided with 78 Flash fixes.

Microsoft warns that AI discovery will keep driving record update volumes; July 2026 again broke the record (#1087).

July 2026 fixed 570 vulnerabilities, 59 critical, the third record month in a row (#1088).

September 2026 set a record with 974 fixes (114 critical, 20 wormable), pushing 2026 past 2,600 (#1096).

October 2026 set another record with 1,411 fixes and introduced experimental "risk cohorts" (#1101).
