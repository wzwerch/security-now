# Global Privacy Control

# Summary

Only Brave, DuckDuckGo and Tor send it by default; California AB 566 will require browsers to offer it from 2027. California's DROP platform adds one-stop data-broker deletion.

California, Colorado and Connecticut attorneys general announced a joint probe of companies ignoring GPC in September 2025.

Episode 934 explains GPC through Yahoo's response, which states honor it and which browsers support it; later episodes note slow mobile support and Engadget's pop-up.

Legally recognized in California and, as laws phase in, Colorado and Connecticut; supported by Firefox and extensions like OptMeowt.
