# Infostealer malware

# Summary

SN 1083 enumerates what the AUR infostealer targets: browser stores, chat apps, developer tokens, SSH and VPN material.

Central to Beaumont's Recall demo and the Snowflake breaches via stolen single-factor credentials; mitigation is mandatory MFA.
