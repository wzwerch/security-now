# Federated Learning of Cohorts

# Summary

Browsers compute a cohort ID locally from recent history, shared with sites, recomputed weekly. EFF warns of fingerprinting and discrimination; Steve is cautiously open.

FLoC placed users into coarse interest cohorts computed in the browser from about a week of browsing. EFF, Vivaldi, Brave, Edge, Opera, Firefox and Amazon rejected or blocked it, and Google delayed wider rollout to around 2023. Steve found it preferable to cookies but saw IP addresses and fingerprinting as larger problems.
