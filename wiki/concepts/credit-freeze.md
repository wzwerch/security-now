# Credit freeze

# Summary

Free by federal law; bureaus allow temporary lifts with automatic re-freeze.

Steve recommends freezing at all bureaus by default (freeze, not lock) given repeated mass breaches, with timed re-freeze when thawing.

Free in the US after the NPD breach; Innovis is a fourth bureau; Experian account flaws and Canada's patchwork discussed.

Steve and Padre compared freezes and fraud alerts.

Steve repeated the recommendation after the Experian breach and criticised fees for freezing.

Steve froze all three for about 10 dollars each in California; bureaus are independent so all three must be frozen.

Steve again urges freezing (not "locking") credit as the main personal defense against identity theft (#1102).
