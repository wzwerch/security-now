# BlueKeep

# Summary

BlueKeep affects Windows XP, 7 and Server 2003/2008 and was patched in May 2019, even for unsupported systems. Researchers confirmed private exploits and about 950,000 exposed hosts, yet Steve expected cryptominer takeovers rather than a worm.

Public exploit code arrived in 2019 and mass exploitation began in November installing Monero miners, without worm behavior.
