# Least privilege

# Summary

Coinbase contractor breach and BPO over-trust illustrate it; also DigiCert's PAM gaps.

Standard-user advice against Rootpipe and Mac firmware flaws.

Limited users, DropMyRights on XP and constrained browser tokens reduce exploit impact.
