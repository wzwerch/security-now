# Unwanted tracking detection

# Summary

Defines near-owner and separated modes, rotating private BLE addresses, delayed sound alerts on movement, encrypted serial lookup and an owner pairing registry accessible to law enforcement.

Car thieves tagged vehicles with AirTags; Apple's Tracker Detect app for Android was criticised.
