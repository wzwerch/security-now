# Residential proxy

# Summary

Bright Data-style SDKs trade bandwidth for perks, while botnets like AVrecon/SocksEscort and Kimwolf turn routers and TV boxes into proxies; the market grows as IP-based blocking increases.

Used to defeat geofencing and attribution; Steve advises isolating IoT devices.

Bright Data SDK in smart TVs; Asocks 17-million-device botnet takedown.
