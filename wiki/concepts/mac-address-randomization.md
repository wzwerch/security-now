# MAC address randomization

# Summary

iOS 14 uses a different address per access point, which also makes open-WiFi logging useless.

A US Naval Academy study found it defeated in about 96 percent of Android phones and bypassable on iPhones via control frames.

iOS 8 randomised only in deep sleep, so Apple's claim was overstated; unique MACs remain valuable in enterprise.
