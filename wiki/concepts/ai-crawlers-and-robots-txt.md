# AI crawlers and robots.txt

# Summary

robots.txt is voluntary (RFC 9309). Perplexity's alleged evasion, AI Overviews cutting traffic, licensing deals, and copyright suits show an unsettled web economy; some small sites even welcome scraping.

OpenAI, Google, Microsoft and Anthropic will honor a shared robots.txt token "__usage_ai-training" proposed at the IETF by Anthropic's Kevin Kelley (#1103).
