# Chat Control

# Summary

Denmark revived it in 2025 but the October 14 vote was cancelled after Germany and the Netherlands opposed it; by March 2026 the EU only extended a voluntary scanning exception to August 2027 with limits. Steve argues content checks, if any, belong in the operating system.

Germany said it will not support breaking encryption, the Danish presidency prioritises the file, Austria planned court-approved messenger monitoring from 2027, and the UK pressed Apple for access. Steve thinks it cannot work without breaking encryption for everyone.

The EU proposal would make detection of CSAM and grooming mandatory with an EU Centre issuing indicator databases; the UK bill similarly threatens encryption. Signal and others say they would leave rather than comply.

The April 2024 draft kept detection of known and new material and grooming text, exempted non-public government and military services, and was expected to pass in June. Steve calls the aims technically infeasible; the UK avoided a similar mandate with a technical-feasibility clause.
