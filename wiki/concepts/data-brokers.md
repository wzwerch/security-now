# Data brokers and DROP

# Summary

DROP launched January 1, 2026 and requires brokers to process deletion requests from August 1, 2026; Steve enrolled and found 170 registered brokers. GM's driving-data sales were an automotive example.

Brookings noted brokers hold up to 1,500 data points per American, so a TikTok ban would not eliminate foreign access to such data.

LexisNexis and Verisk driver reports, MOAB aggregation and law-enforcement purchases show the market's scope.

NPD and MC2 Data exposures; PayPal's data sharing; Steve proposes bonding and insurance for aggregators.

Acxiom, Datalogix, Epsilon and BlueKai in Facebook ads.

DROP deletions became mandatory on 1 August 2026 for 662 registered brokers; Steve's request shows 393 still pending (#1096).
