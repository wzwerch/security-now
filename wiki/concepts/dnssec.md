# DNSSEC

# Summary

Steve clarifies DNSSEC does not encrypt and works alongside DNSCrypt, DNS over TLS and DNS over HTTPS.

Steve explained why it took 20 years and why the root KSK rollover was postponed; adoption figures were given.

Slow deployment limits DANE and counters DNS blocking like Turkey's Twitter ban.

Rarely deployed because DNS works without it; Steve notes it could allow DNS-based certificate verification.
