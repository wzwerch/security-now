# Fuzzing

# Summary

AI now builds fuzzers and harnesses in days, as with FatFs and OpenAI's Daybreak.

Check Point audited RDP clients by hand; NIST improved combinatorial coverage testing.

FuzzBench, the Mayhem find in OpenWrt, the USBFuzz results and TRRespass illustrated fuzzing across software and hardware.

Project Zero's DOM fuzzer found bugs in all five major browsers.
