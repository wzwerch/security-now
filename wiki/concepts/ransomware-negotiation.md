# Ransomware negotiation and escrow

# Summary

KELA reported rising demand for English-speaking negotiators, while Steve predicted escrow services and reputation systems among ransomware affiliates. Firms like Coveware negotiate on the victim side.

GuidePoint reports LLM-written, unusually precise ransom negotiations and fake "legal counsel" claims (#1088).
