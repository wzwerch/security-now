# Privacy Sandbox

# Summary

Interest groups and ad auctions run inside the browser, with fenced frames isolating the ad; advertisers do not learn the visitor's interests.

Steve argues ad targeting without tracking is a reasonable compromise.

Topics API trials expand from September 2022 with launch targeted for Q3 2023 and cookie removal no earlier than the second half of 2024.
