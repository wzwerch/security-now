# Secure by Design

# Summary

Interfaces should be off by default, refuse unsafe exposure and warn before defaults are loosened; echoed by the EU Cyber Resilience Act.

No default credentials, physical confirmation for risky changes, minimal exposed services, auto-updates and published support lifetimes; a physical button should be edge triggered, though a bypassable control still beats none.
