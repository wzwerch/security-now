# Summary

Explained with Wally, locked ballot boxes and Ali Baba's cave; properties are completeness, soundness and zero knowledge. Relevant to age verification.

Steve explained in feedback why simply hashing purchase quantities fails because the small input space allows brute force, motivating the locked-box protocol he described earlier.
