# DNS over HTTPS

# Summary

Browsers favor DoH; Cloudflare's DoH stayed up in the 1.1.1.1 outage.

CISA told agencies to avoid third-party DoH, Windows 10 previewed OS-level DoH for Cloudflare, Google and Quad9, and Firefox's automatic rollout overloaded NextDNS and a county proxy.

Google offered it, Mozilla and Cloudflare piloted it in Firefox 60, and Cloudflare's 1.1.1.1 also supports DNS over TLS on port 853.
