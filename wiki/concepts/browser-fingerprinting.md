# Browser fingerprinting

# Summary

EFF's Cover Your Tracks showed Steve unique among 244,246 browsers.

EFF's Cover Your Tracks showed Steve's Firefox as unique (18.2 bits) while Brave and Safari (iOS 26 default) randomize. Defaults matter more than options.

The FPTrace study showed that changing a fingerprint alters ad bids and cookie syncing, including after consent opt-outs, with recognised users drawing about 40% higher bids. Firefox 143 added more fingerprinting protection, though EFF Cover Your Tracks still sees it as unique.

Episodes covered Gummy Browsers replay, Bluetooth radio fingerprinting and GPU timing.
