# Border device searches

# Summary

A Boston federal court held suspicionless warrantless searches unconstitutional in November 2019; secondary inspections still allow some searches.

SN 611 recounts a US citizen pressured at SFO; Leo and Steve suggest travel with a wiped or cheap phone.
