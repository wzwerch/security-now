# UK technical capability notice

# Summary

Issued under the Investigatory Powers Act 2016 and a criminal offence to confirm. Led to US congressional pushback, Apple withdrawing ADP for new UK users and an appeal. Google declined to deny it received one. Listeners coined 'master key' as a better term than 'backdoor'.
