# BGP Route Leak

# Summary

The June 2019 leak sent about 2 percent of traffic through a steel company because a provider accepted unfiltered prefixes. Prefix limits and route filtering are cheap mitigations.

Telekom Malaysia's mistaken announcements overloaded its links and hurt Level 3.
