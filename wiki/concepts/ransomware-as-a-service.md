# Ransomware as a Service

# Summary

GandCrab exemplified the model, taking a small commission to attract affiliates; Steve contrasts it with high-end targeted ransomware such as Ryuk.

Intel 471 counted 25+ RaaS portals; REvil took 20-30% cuts and recruited affiliates.

SN 610 covers Karmen, a $175 kit with a dashboard.

Ransom32 was offered this way in January 2016.
