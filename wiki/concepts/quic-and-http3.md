# QUIC and HTTP/3

# Summary

QUIC folds connection setup, TLS 1.3 and HTTP/2 ideas into a single exchange, enabling one-packet requests, and was named HTTP/3 by the IETF.

Introduced as Quick UDP Internet Connections.
