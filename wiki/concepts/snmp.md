# SNMP

# Summary

Designed in 1988 without security, SNMPv3 adds crypto but is often unused. 2.3 million Cisco devices answered SNMP from the Internet to an exploited IOS bug.

NetWorx can monitor the whole network by reading the router's SNMP byte counters.
