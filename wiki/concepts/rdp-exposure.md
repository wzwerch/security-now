# Exposed Remote Desktop

# Summary

Reports ranked RDP above phishing as an initial vector, with exposed ports jumping during COVID and low-skilled actors using Masscan and brute-force tools; advice is VPN with MFA, IP allow-listing or SSH tunnelling.
