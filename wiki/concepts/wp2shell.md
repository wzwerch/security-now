# wp2shell

# Summary

Introduced in 6.9.0 (December 2025), found by Assetnote; WordPress shipped 7.0.2 and 6.9.5 with forced automatic updates (#1088).

Found with GPT 5.6 Sol by Searchlight Cyber; exploited in the wild with persistent web shells within days (#1089).
