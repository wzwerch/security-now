# Backdoor versus incompetence

# Summary

Intent cannot be proven; real backdoors would be better hidden. Examples: old Cisco hard-coded accounts, the Windows metafile escape, and the ESP32 undocumented HCI commands. The term 'backdoor' is also criticised for lawful-access mandates, which are 'front doors' or 'master keys'.
