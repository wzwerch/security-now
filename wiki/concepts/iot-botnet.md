# IoT botnet

# Summary

A smart washing machine uploading gigabytes daily illustrates unnoticed botnet membership; isolate IoT on a guest LAN.

Mirai variants and novice botnet operators with default credentials.

Brickerbot, Hajime and Mirai show destructive and vigilante responses.

Mirai and Reaper illustrate the risk of unpatched devices.

Satori and Mirai variants exploit routers; light bulbs are too weak for mining.
