# Two-factor authentication

# Summary

SMS is weakest (SIM swapping, Twitter's paywalling); authenticator apps and hardware keys are stronger; Google gave away Titan keys; MFA fatigue is an emerging attack on push approvals.

Twitter's RSA challenge scheme with hash-chain backup codes; Authenticator loss on iOS.
