# Facial Recognition Surveillance

# Summary

The FBI and ICE ran hundreds of thousands of searches against state databases, prompting bipartisan congressional criticism and San Francisco's municipal ban.

Topics included London trials with up to 90 percent false positives, masks defeating recognition, IBM's exit and the ACLU's case against Clearview AI.
