# AES-GCM

# Summary

Zoom moved from AES-ECB, which leaks patterns, to AES-256-GCM in version 5.0, though keys were still generated by its servers until end-to-end encryption arrives.

Steve chose GCM for SQRL and wrote a public-domain C version tested with 47,250 NIST vectors; IVs must never repeat.
