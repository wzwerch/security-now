# Ransomware and data extortion

# Summary

Includes the Salesforce leak deadline, Jaguar Land Rover's month-long shutdown without cyber insurance, ransom payment statistics and the DigitalMint negotiator indicted for attacking his own clients.

Seen in the US Marshals breach, school districts' weakness, and Cl0p's mass theft campaign.

Change Healthcare, LockBit, payment trends (29 percent paid in Q4 2023) and Cybereason's survey feature in the batch.

Gangs added searchable leak databases; sanctions pushed groups to rebrand; Conti dissolved into other crews.

The Seven Deadly Sins says it deploys no ransomware and only steals and extorts, reflecting the shift away from encryption (#1098).
