# Large language models

# Summary

Steve moved from calling LLMs an illusion of intelligence (Wizard of Oz) to retracting that after studying pre-training, the 2017 Transformer paper and test-time compute behind o1/o3. Model collapse and AI crawlers were related topics.

Steve's view in 2023: impressive simulation of intelligence, useful for recombining known code, unable to analyse novel complex malware; he expects it to transform search.
