# SSD slow-region repair

# Summary

ReadSpeed exposes error-correction slowdowns; rewriting aligned chunks restored speed on an OCZ Vertex 3, supporting a future SpinRite for SSDs.

A 4 TB Western Digital SSD's read slowdowns vanished over the part SpinRite rewrote (#1094).
