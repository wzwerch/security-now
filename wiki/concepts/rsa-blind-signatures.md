# RSA blind signatures

# Summary

Google One VPN uses them so the VPN cannot link a connection to a subscriber.

A September 2026 key-forgery attack reduces blind-signature RSA to about 65/90/119 bits of security for 1024/2048/4096-bit keys, affecting Privacy Pass-style systems (#1099).
