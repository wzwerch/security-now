# Bug bounty

# Summary

AI slop and discovery volume led to an IBB pause and curl and Node.js ending payouts.

Examples: Zoom payouts, Google Mobile VRP, Chrome full-chain bonus up to $180,000, and legal defense funds.

Pentagon and DHS Log4j rounds paid 500 to 5,000 dollars; the EU funded 200,000 euros; Zerodium pays far more to resell.

HackerOne's insider case, a $50,000 Microsoft award for the SIKE break and Apple's $2M Lockdown Mode bounty.

HackerOne payout milestones and bounty flow by country were discussed.

Intel, Microsoft, Netflix, Dropbox and the DoD all expanded programs in early 2018; Steve sees a career path for researchers.
