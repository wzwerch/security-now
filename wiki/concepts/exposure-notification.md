# Exposure notification

# Summary

Devices broadcast rotating identifiers derived from daily keys; positive users upload daily diagnosis keys and other phones check locally for matches. Steve judged the cryptography sound but argued voluntary adoption would be too low and that health authorities need location data, while Schneier doubted usefulness.
