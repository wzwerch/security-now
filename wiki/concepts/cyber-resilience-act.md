# EU Cyber Resilience Act

# Summary

Vulnerability reporting, five-year support, default automatic updates and attack reporting, effective about three years after adoption.

Named in the EU's 2026 Action Plan on Cybersecurity and AI alongside NIS2, DORA and the AI Act (#1087).
