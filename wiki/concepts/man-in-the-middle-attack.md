# Man-in-the-middle attack

# Summary

Episode 937 explains why active interception is harder than eavesdropping, and episode 941's Egypt case shows plain HTTP injection delivering spyware.

Facebook's research app, antivirus scanning and Sea Turtle show legitimate and malicious forms.

Central to Samsung keyboard, AFNetworking, Logjam and plain-HTTP script injection.
