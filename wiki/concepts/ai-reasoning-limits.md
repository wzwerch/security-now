# AI reasoning limits

# Summary

Apple's Illusion of Thinking study found reasoning models collapse at high puzzle complexity even when given the algorithm. Steve frames LLMs as linguistic simulations of intelligence, invokes the ELIZA effect, and notes behaviour is hard to control or edit.
