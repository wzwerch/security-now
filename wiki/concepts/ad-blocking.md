# Ad blocking

# Summary

Impressions pay sites, intrusive ads drive blocking, and carriers and VPN apps now block at network level.

Ongoing 2015 debate about ethics, publisher revenue, perverse incentives, acceptable ads, first-party ads proxied through sites, and the launch of iOS 9 content blockers.

2015 coverage included iOS content blockers (1Blocker, Purify, Focus), anti-adblock notices and services, DNS-level blocking and the bandwidth cost of ads.
