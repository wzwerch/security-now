# Key escrow

# Summary

Blaze's testimony stressed new failure points; Admiral Rogers floated split-key ideas.

Steve distinguished per-device random keys held by a vendor from a single master backdoor; both create a high-value target. Microsoft's automatic disk-key upload is a deployed example.

Steve argued this does not weaken the cryptography itself, only adds key management burden and policy risk; Skipjack is the precedent, and he says realistic legislation would make vendors keep ghost keys.
