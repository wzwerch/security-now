# Open-weight models

# Summary

GLM 5.2 enabled Hugging Face's forensics when hosted models refused; Kimi K3 brings near-frontier capability, though it needs large GPU clusters to run (#1089).

Refusal training in open models can be stripped by abliteration; abliterated versions of most open models circulate (#1092).

GLM-5.3 makes Mythos-class offensive capability freely available; its safeguards fall to role-play, prefilled reasoning or abliteration (#1099).
