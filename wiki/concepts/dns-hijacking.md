# DNS and domain hijacking

# Summary

Sitting Ducks lets attackers claim lame-delegated domains; Ether.fi lost registrar control for three hours; Steve urges MFA at the registrar.

A capability of the ZuoRAT router implant; with a certificate it can defeat TLS warnings.

Sea Turtle compromised registrars to redirect victims; ICANN urged DNSSEC.

SN 699 covered the Iran-linked campaign and CISA's emergency directive requiring audits, MFA and certificate transparency monitoring; automated Let's Encrypt issuance makes DNS the weak link.
