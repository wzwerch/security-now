# IPv4 address exhaustion

# Summary

Prices reached $50 to $60 per address in 2022 while IPv6 stays free and unwanted.

Transfers fragment routing tables; the UK government and Romanian holders sold blocks.

ARIN began its unmet-requests policy in July 2015 with only /23 and /24 blocks left.

LACNIC and Microsoft Azure exhaustion stories.
