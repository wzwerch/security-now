# Trapdoored primes

# Summary

Researchers solved a 1024-bit discrete logarithm in two months on a few thousand cores by hiding structure in the prime; an honest prime is estimated at least 10,000 times harder. Steve says the danger applies to fixed, unexplained primes in standards and recommends 2048-bit sizes and verifiable provenance of magic numbers.
