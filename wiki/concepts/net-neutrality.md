# Net Neutrality

# Summary

In 2017 the FCC proposed reversing Title II classification; Steve and Leo urged listeners to comment via GoFCCYourself.com and DearFCC.org.

FCC rollback debate, Verizon video throttling, 21.8 million comments with 98.5 percent of unique ones supporting the rules, and Steve's preference for Congress to settle it.

The FCC planned repeal in December 2017; zero-rating bundles were discussed.

State procurement orders and a 22-state lawsuit pushed back on the FCC repeal; apps like OONI measure violations.

Washington and Oregon passed state laws in 2018, Oregon via public purchasing requirements.

Brett Glass and Leo argued two-sided markets and Title II; later episodes tie it to peering congestion.
