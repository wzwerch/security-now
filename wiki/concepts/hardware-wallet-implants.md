# Hardware wallet implants

# Summary

In October 2026 implanted Ledger devices resold via CryptoBilis sent 24-word phrases over GSM, enabling a $102 million theft from 335 wallets; Joe Grand found implants in Trezor devices too; SeedSigner or Blockstream Jade let users build their own (#1101).
