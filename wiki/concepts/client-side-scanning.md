# Client-side scanning

# Summary

The mechanism behind Chat Control proposals; critics see it as surveillance, and Steve suggests on-device or OS-level local AI as the least bad design if it must exist.

The fallback compliance route under the UK Online Safety Bill.

Apple explained in ep. 938 why it dropped iCloud CSAM scanning; the UK clause in ep. 939 limits mandates to technically feasible, accredited tech.

A letter from 312 scientists argued targeted detection still yields millions of false positives and undermines encryption.

Google and Apple blur sensitive images on device; Steve argues local AI could end the encryption backdoor debate, with slippery-slope concerns.
