# WordPress plugin vulnerabilities

# Summary

Jetpack's silently force-patched flaw versus the Fancy Product Designer zero-day (no auto-update) show the importance of update channels.

PHP Everywhere, UpdraftPlus and Elementor, plus Patchstack statistics, support Steve's advice to keep WordPress minimal.
