# Exposed internet-facing services

# Summary

Webmin, RDP and QNAP cases show internet exposure plus slow patching drives mass exploitation.

Examples include Patreon's debugger, a MongoDB with MacKeeper customer data and cable modem backdoors, all findable through Shodan.
