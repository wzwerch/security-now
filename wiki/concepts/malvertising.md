# Malvertising

# Summary

Chains of scripts across ad networks lead to exploit kits and ransomware on major sites with no user interaction; blockers and click-to-play plugins reduce exposure.

Steve explained why ads, not user uploads, are the favoured vector: they may carry script and Flash on high-traffic sites.

A March 2016 Angler campaign touched major news sites; advice is to remove unused plugins and use content blockers.

384,000 malicious ads in 2013; most evade antivirus; block scripts.

A Zedo/DoubleClick ad hit sites like Last.fm with a Zemot downloader.
