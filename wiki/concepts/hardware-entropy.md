# Hardware entropy sources

# Summary

Cloudflare hashes lava-lamp camera images; Steve notes real-world sensors give good entropy once whitened.

Mixing Intel's hardware RNG with kernel events cannot reduce randomness.

Reverse-biased diode and quantum USB generators; GRC will seed its password page from one.
