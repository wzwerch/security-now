# Third-party cookies

# Summary

Firefox partitions cookies per site; Chrome began disabling them in 2024.

Steve predicts sites will force first-party sign-ups; he praises the Sandbox design while Google delayed removal to 2025 pending UK CMA review. Studies show 65 percent of sites ignore cookie rejections.

Google delayed, then abandoned, deprecation after UK CMA review, noyb's complaint and industry pushback; Chrome will instead offer a user choice.

W3C TAG says remove them; Firefox's Total Cookie Protection and UI confusion; Firefox 132 fixes Strict mode.

Firefox partitions them, Chrome keeps delaying removal, and Steve doubts anti-tracking can succeed without regulation.

Trackers flooding cookies evicted login cookies; Safari blocks them by default.

Safari blocks, Firefox planned; sites that break.

Firefox 23 did not block them; keep until exit option; iframe use cases.
