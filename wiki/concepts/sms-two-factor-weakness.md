# SMS as second factor

# Summary

SS7 interception makes SMS weak; use TOTP or security keys.

Reddit and Instagram moved to authenticator apps after account takeovers; convenience keeps SMS popular.

Yahoo's SMS-only on-demand login is something you are sent, not something you have, and is vulnerable to malware, wiretaps and device seizure.

GM is switching users from authenticator apps back to SMS or email codes (#1088).
