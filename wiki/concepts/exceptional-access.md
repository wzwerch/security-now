# Exceptional access (encryption backdoors)

# Summary

A 2015 paper by eleven cryptographers argued it would undo forward secrecy and raise insoluble jurisdiction questions; India floated and withdrew a plaintext-retention policy.

Matthew Green predicts AI-hardened software will revive demands for built-in backdoors as exploits dry up (#1095).
