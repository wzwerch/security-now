# IPv6 transition

# Summary

Only about a third of users reach IPv6-only services; US adoption flat since 2019; NAT and SNI let IPv4 scale.

Steve expects NAT-like filtering to persist even with IPv6.

Adoption reached about 10 percent by 2016; Steve expects stateful firewalls to replace NAT's accidental protection.
