# Ransomware

# Summary

Steve argues insurer payouts keep it lucrative and takedowns cause rebranding; payments may violate sanctions; the Kuala Lumpur airport refused a 10 million USD demand; RansomLook.io tracks victims.

The CDK Global case illustrates untested backups and over-dependence on one SaaS provider.

REvil takedown efforts, Khonsari and Conti using Log4Shell, and insurance effects.

Episodes cover CommonRansom, SamSam sanctions, Hidden Tear derivatives and free decryptors.

In late 2015 it shifted to servers (Linux.Encoder), had buggy variants that could not decrypt (Power Worm) and gained cross-platform JavaScript kits (Ransom32).

CryptoLocker's flawless crypto and 72-hour countdown.

CryptoLocker as the template: strong crypto, bitcoin payment, domain generation, imitators.
