# Thunderbolt DMA attacks (Thunderspy)

# Summary

Researchers found seven flaws, including firmware checked only at write time, cloneable device identities and unprotected configuration, allowing DMA access past disk encryption and lock screens with a few minutes of physical access.

USB 3.0 preferred.
