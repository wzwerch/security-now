# Lawful access

# Summary

Chat Control, Ireland's interception law, Canada Bill C-22, BitLocker key disclosure.

Discussed via the EU child-protection regulation and the ghost-participant approach GCHQ outlined.

UK and EU proposals, FBI comments and a leaked UK technical capability notice draft appear; Steve expects per-company warrant compliance rather than a golden key.

Australia's 2018 bill and Signal's response, plus Steve's per-device escrow idea for single-vendor ecosystems.

Debate over a front door versus a backdoor with industry and experts opposing mandates.

The 2015 episodes track Obama administration positions, Comey's remarks, a Manhattan DA proposal and Steve's per-device escrow idea, with repeated points that encryption is math and backdoors leak.
