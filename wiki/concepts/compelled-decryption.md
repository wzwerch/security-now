# Compelled device unlocking

# Summary

Courts split on whether entering a passcode is testimonial; an Oregon appellate court applied the foregone-conclusion exception while a Florida court demanded more specific knowledge of evidence.

The Pennsylvania Supreme Court held 4-3 that revealing a password is testimonial under the Fifth Amendment, while biometrics may be treated as physical evidence.

A Massachusetts ruling compelled a password; a Virginia ruling protected passcodes but allowed fingerprints.
