# Egress filtering and anti-spoofing

# Summary

Steve repeatedly notes that missing source-address validation enables reflection attacks.

Steve names it the general cure for reflection attacks.

Steve says ISPs should do it to stop SYN floods and reflection attacks; only inertia prevents it.
