# Summary

Steve follows up on DarkSide. Mandiant's report describes the affiliate model (operators take a sliding 10-25% cut, affiliates are interviewed and given a polished Tor admin panel, prohibited targets include hospitals and CIS countries), and DarkSide announced it lost infrastructure and was shutting down, which some call an exit scam. Elliptic's blockchain analysis traced Colonial Pipeline's 75 bitcoin payment and others to one wallet that received about $17.5 million and was emptied afterward. The two main Russian-language criminal forums, xss.is and exploit.in, banned ransomware advertising and were then hit by DDoS. Steve points to a DarkTracer list of 2,200+ ransomware victims, which he mirrored as a PDF. May Patch Tuesday was modest (55 fixes) but included a potentially wormable IIS http.sys bug in recent Windows 10 server builds with a public proof of concept, a 9.9 Hyper-V flaw, and more Exchange fixes. After a Doppler-shift explanation of last week's cartoon and sci-fi and UAP chatter, the main topic is FragAttacks from KRACK researcher Mathy Vanhoef: three design flaws (unauthenticated "is aggregated" flag, mixed-key fragment reassembly, fragment cache not flushed) and several implementation bugs such as accepting plaintext fragments, affecting WEP through WPA3. Steve's advice is not to panic: attackers need radio range, HTTPS blunts most impact, and IoT devices should sit on a separate network.

# Key points

- Mandiant on DarkSide: RaaS launched by "darksupp" around Nov 2020 on xss.is and exploit.in; DarkSide 2.0 (April 2021) added automated test decryption and DDoS; Windows and Linux builds, Salsa20 plus RSA-1024; affiliates take most of the proceeds; at least five Russian-speaking affiliates, some also working with REvil or Babuk; extortion tactics included offering leaks to stock traders and using stolen cyber-insurance policy details in negotiation.
- May 13 announcement that DarkSide lost servers and would close; Toshiba Tec France was nonetheless hit by DarkSide (740 GB claimed), showing operator and affiliate are decoupled.
- Elliptic: wallet that received Colonial's 75 BTC (May 8) had 57 payments from 21 wallets, including 78.29 BTC from chemical distributor Brenntag; it was emptied around May 13-14; cash-out via exchanges and the Hydra marketplace.
- Bitcoin and Tor offer only apparent anonymity; mixers and chain hopping complicate tracing but blockchain analytics remain effective.
- Brian Krebs noted DarkSide-style malware skips systems with Russian or CIS keyboard layouts (a weak mitigation Steve does not endorse relying on).
- xss.is and exploit.in banned ransomware-as-a-service topics citing too much attention and political pressure; Steve expects the trade to continue more quietly.
- Patch Tuesday May 2021: 55 fixes; CVE-2021-31166 (IIS http.sys use-after-free RCE, CVSS 9.8, Windows 10 2004/20H2 only, PoC on GitHub); a Hyper-V RCE rated 9.9; more Exchange fixes (third month running); IE scripting engine flaw.
- FragAttacks (USENIX 2021, Black Hat): aggregation flag unauthenticated (fix not backward compatible), mixed-key fragments, fragment cache; implementation flaws include accepting unencrypted fragments, broadcast fragments, handshake-like aggregated frames, and not checking that fragments belong to one frame. Disclosure took nine months under Wi-Fi Alliance and ICASI supervision.
- Practical impact: attacker must be within radio range; most dangerous use is injecting packets to attack poorly patched home IoT devices; mitigations are HTTPS, DNS over HTTPS, updates and network segmentation.
- Listener FLoC cohort ID changed from 5393 to 6501 in a few weeks.
