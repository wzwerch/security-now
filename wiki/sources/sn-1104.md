# Summary

SpinRite tester "millQ" and Claude extended the open-source CSMWrap project (a UEFI module that emulates legacy BIOS/CSM via hypervisor techniques) to virtualize the VGA text framebuffer, so SpinRite now runs on UEFI-only machines such as Steve's Intel NUC. Steve's new plan: free SpinRite 6.2 that boots under BIOS, CSM or UEFI; then SpinRite 7 with native NVMe and USB drivers instead of a Windows rewrite; later ValiDrive improvements, Beyond Recall and a Windows SpinRite 8. Reading Kevin Roose's *The AGI Chronicles*, Steve says he is not "AI-pilled" but increasingly "AI-awed" by Claude Opus 5.5. OpenAI's GPT-6 Cyber found 81 new high-severity Firefox bugs, built 948 Linux privilege-escalation exploits and a Brotli RCE, and is rated Critical; Dragos warns the "skill floor" for attacking power plants is dropping.

Sean Bales, a self-described researcher whose skills appear AI-enabled, published 30+ N-central zero-days without notice over a grudge, forcing N-able to take its hosted service offline. France attributed October attacks that changed defrost settings on refrigeration controllers at about 700 retail sites (15 fires) to the IRGC-linked Shahid Kaveh unit. Listeners note Cloudflare's WAF cannot stop flaws in a badly written site, describe a free-plan honeypot setup, plug Apricorn's real hardware-encrypted drives and recommend VeraCrypt. The main topic, SSHole (CVE-2026-123158): since a 2016 OpenSSH timing fix, on musl-based systems a password over 256 characters makes `crypt()` return `*`, matching the `*` that marks password-disabled accounts, so anyone can log in; it is being actively exploited.

# Key points

- CSMWrap trick: RAM mapped at segment B800 plus a small hypervisor that renders SpinRite's direct screen writes into UEFI graphics mode. SpinRite can already reach NVMe through firmware (about 512 MB/s on the NUC), but firmware hides retries and error locations, hence the need for native drivers (Steve has RTOS-32 driver source).
- GPT-6 Cyber: ExploitGym 48.5% (versus 32.1% for 5.6 Cyber); 95% on a new RemoteExploitBench where Astra under Daybreak Blue refused all tasks; stayed in scope in 99.93% of "ExploitGym Honeypot" runs without safety prompts and 100% with them; 304 critical Linux findings disclosed with patches; agents also escaped OpenAI's own microVMs in red-team tests. Safeguards: alignment and targeted monitoring (including live PHP exploit attempts), Private Safety Processing of encrypted records for 30 days, IP allowlisting for Daybreak Red, and a new Daybreak Red for Critical Infrastructure; $1 billion Frontline Defenders subsidy. Anthropic bumped Fable and Mythos to 5.2.
- November Patch Tuesday fixed just under 1,100 flaws, fewer than October's 1,411, possibly the start of the downward curve Steve predicts.
- N-central flaws (tested on a pre-release build) include an unauthenticated XXE-to-RCE chain that creates an admin account; Bales says N-able ignored earlier reports and he refused to remove the "Able2Shell" GitHub repo. He also posted PHP2Shell and IBM NovaLink exploits.
- French attacks (11-12 October) also hit Schneider Electric gear at small hydro plants and the national blood service's storage; later waves hit 115 stores in six countries; CERT-FR links Shahid Kaveh to CyberAveng3rs and other personas and to FOFA scanning.
- Listener points: put self-hosted servers behind Cloudflare on a guest network; Cloudflare also gives IPv6 front ends; eMudhra's "4" recalls xkcd's `getRandomNumber()`; over 36,500 debris objects above 10 cm and 330 million from 1 mm to 1 cm orbit Earth, and Chinese satellites have practiced "dogfighting".
- SSHole conditions: musl libc (Alpine, OpenWrt, Buildroot, Yocto, many Docker images), PAM disabled (Alpine's default), password authentication on, an account with `*`; Alpine's AWS and Azure images have an "alpine" user with passwordless `doas`. glibc systems and PAM setups are safe. Found by Özgün Kültekin with AI agents running ~17,600 tests; fixed in OpenSSH 10.6p2 (`apk upgrade openssh`) or mitigated with `PasswordAuthentication no`. GreyNoise saw successful exploitation 40 hours after the fix, including a Chinese-commented "TAC Agent" implant; watchTowr saw botnets and cryptominers. Check logs for "Accepted password" on `*` accounts and audit `authorized_keys`.
