# Encryption and lawful-access fights, 2016

# Overview

Governments pushing for decryption capability: BlackBerry, the FBI/Apple case, Burr-Feinstein, Brazil and Russia.

# Timeline

* **#556 (2016-04-19)** — RCMP is revealed to have held BlackBerry's global consumer key; the FBI finds little on the San Bernardino iPhone. ([source](../sources/sn-0556.md))
* **#557 (2016-04-26)** — Comey implies the unlock cost over a million dollars; both FBI court cases end in setbacks. ([source](../sources/sn-0557.md))
* **#558 (2016-05-03)** — The Snowden/Zakaria debate shifts opinion slightly toward lawful access; courts remain split on compelled unlocking. ([source](../sources/sn-0558.md))
* **#562 (2016-05-31)** — Burr-Feinstein effectively dies, with intelligence agencies ambivalent. ([source](../sources/sn-0562.md))
* **#567 (2016-07-05)** — A Brazilian court freezes about $6 million of Facebook funds over WhatsApp's undecryptable messages. ([source](../sources/sn-0567.md))
* **#568 (2016-07-12)** — Russia mandates data retention and decryption assistance; Private Internet Access pulls out of Russia. ([source](../sources/sn-0568.md))
* **#569 (2016-07-19)** — WhatsApp is blocked in Brazil again. ([source](../sources/sn-0569.md))
