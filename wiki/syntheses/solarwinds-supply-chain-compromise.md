# SolarWinds Orion supply chain compromise

# Overview

FireEye's disclosure and the unfolding Sunburst and Supernova story.

# Timeline

* **#797 (2020-12-15)** — FireEye revealed its breach, then tied it to trojanized signed Orion updates from March 2020; SolarWinds said 18,000 customers installed them and Russian involvement was reported. ([source](../sources/sn-0797.md))
* **#799 (2020-12-29)** — A separate Supernova backdoor, an October 2019 test run, a Microsoft/FireEye/GoDaddy kill switch and decoded DNS logs naming many additional victims emerged. ([source](../sources/sn-0799.md))
