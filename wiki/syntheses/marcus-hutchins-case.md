# Marcus Hutchins arrest and case

# Overview

Timeline of the WannaCry sinkhole researcher's arrest and indictment.

# Timeline

* **#623 (2017-08-08)** — Arrested after DEF CON on Kronos charges; experts call the indictment a stretch, colleagues defend him, and a defense fund is set up. ([source](../sources/sn-0623.md))
* **#624 (2017-08-15)** — Pleads not guilty, is released to stay in the US without access to the sinkhole, with trial set for October. ([source](../sources/sn-0624.md))
* **#625 (2017-08-22)** — Six counts and up to 40 years reported; debate over hooking-code similarity to Kronos and speculation that the kill switch was staged. ([source](../sources/sn-0625.md))
* **#626 (2017-08-29)** — Defense fund refunded after most donations proved fraudulent. ([source](../sources/sn-0626.md))
* **#627 (2017-09-05)** — Brian Krebs's research suggests he sold malware as a teen before changing course. ([source](../sources/sn-0627.md))
