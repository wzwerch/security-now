# SharePoint ToolShell zero-day

# Overview

Microsoft's flawed patch of a Pwn2Own SharePoint bug led to mass exploitation of on-premises servers.

# Timeline

* **#1036 (2025-07-29)** — ToolShell (CVE-2025-49704/49706, then 53770/53771) was exploited from July 7 to 19, 2025; over 400 organizations including NNSA, DHS and NIH were hit. Steve stresses rotating ASP.NET machine keys; only SharePoint 2016/2019 got fixes. ([source](../sources/sn-1036.md))
