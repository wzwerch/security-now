# Equifax breach

# Overview

Aftermath of the 2017 Equifax data breach.

# Timeline

* **#628 (2017-09-12)** — Steve summarises the breach of about 143 million US consumers, the five-week disclosure delay, the Struts angle and timestamp-based freeze PINs. ([source](../sources/sn-0628.md))
* **#629 (2017-09-19)** — Fraud alerts versus freezes are compared; the security chief's lack of security background and scrubbed online profile are reported. ([source](../sources/sn-0629.md))
* **#630 (2017-09-26)** — Experian's PIN recovery is shown to be weak and some in the security community suspect a state actor. ([source](../sources/sn-0630.md))
* **#631 (2017-10-03)** — Bloomberg details the March Struts exploit, an entry crew handing off to advanced intruders and over 30 entry points. ([source](../sources/sn-0631.md))
* **#637 (2017-11-14)** — Bruce Schneier's congressional testimony places sole fault on Equifax and calls for data broker regulation. ([source](../sources/sn-0637.md))
