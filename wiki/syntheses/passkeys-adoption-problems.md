# Passkeys adoption problems

# Overview

Portability, slot limits and site support issues for Passkeys.

* **#888 (2022-09-13)** — Steve says passkeys will eventually defeat reverse-proxy phishing. ([source](../sources/sn-0888.md))
* **#893 (2022-10-18)** — passkeys.dev launches with few demo sites. ([source](../sources/sn-0893.md))
* **#895 (2022-11-01)** — PayPal announces passkeys, but Steve cannot find them; PayPal SMS login code bypass noted. ([source](../sources/sn-0895.md))

* **#899 (2022-11-29)** — A listener shows third-party QR flows (OwnID) lose phishing resistance. ([source](../sources/sn-0899.md))
* **#972 (2024-04-30)** — William Brown's Shattered Dream essay criticises platform lock-in; Steve prefers passwords plus TOTP in a cross-platform manager. ([source](../sources/sn-0972.md))
* **#973 (2024-05-07)** — Passkeys cannot be exported or shared; YubiKey holds 25 and Chrome admits platform Passkeys are not recoverable. ([source](../sources/sn-0973.md))
* **#974 (2024-05-14)** — Listeners clarify most sites allow multiple Passkeys but implementations vary and some block Firefox. ([source](../sources/sn-0974.md))
