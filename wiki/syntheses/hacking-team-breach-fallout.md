# Hacking Team breach fallout (2015)

# Overview

Leak of an Italian spyware vendor and the zero-days it exposed.

# Timeline

* **#515 (2015-07-07)** — Hacking Team lost 400 GB of data: FBI purchases, a US front company, weak passwords and an unpatched Flash zero-day with a ready exploit. ([source](../sources/sn-0515.md))
* **#516 (2015-07-14)** — A second Flash zero-day, an Internet Explorer zero-day and a UEFI BIOS rootkit offering were found in the dump; Patch Tuesday fixed several. ([source](../sources/sn-0516.md))
* **#517 (2015-07-21)** — A Windows font-handling zero-day from the leak was patched out of band; research later found weaponized antivirus exploits too (SN 527). ([source](../sources/sn-0517.md))
* **#527 (2015-09-29)** — Tavis Ormandy cited the leak as evidence of a black market in antivirus exploits, noting ESET attacks were for sale. ([source](../sources/sn-0527.md))
