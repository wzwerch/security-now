# Cryptomining malware resurgence (2019)

# Overview

Hadoop, Docker and browser miners and their economics.

# Timeline

* **#687 (2018-10-30)** — DemonBot used unpatched Hadoop YARN for mining and DDoS, and Docker API hosts were infected with miners. ([source](../sources/sn-0687.md))
* **#690 (2018-11-20)** — Juniper saw Docker-targeting Monero miners that scan internal networks. ([source](../sources/sn-0690.md))
* **#697 (2019-01-15)** — A study tied over 4 percent of Monero to illicit mining and Check Point listed miners as top malware. ([source](../sources/sn-0697.md))
* **#733 (2019-09-24)** — A catalogue of 2019 Monero mining campaigns across servers, desktops and Android. ([source](../sources/sn-0733.md))
* **#737 (2019-10-22)** — Turla-attributed WAV steganography delivers a miner or reverse shell. ([source](../sources/sn-0737.md))
* **#739 (2019-11-05)** — BlueKeep attacks install Monero miners. ([source](../sources/sn-0739.md))
