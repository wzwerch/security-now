# Apple and iOS security in mid-2016

# Overview

Patches, bounties, spyware and weaknesses affecting iPhones and iMessage.

# Timeline

* **#570 (2016-07-26)** — Cisco Talos finds image-parsing flaws (TIFF and others) in OS X and iOS, fixed in iOS 9.3.3; Steve urges manual updating and gateway blocking of unneeded file types. ([source](../sources/sn-0570.md))
* **#572 (2016-08-09)** — Apple announces a bug bounty up to $200,000; Steve welcomes it. ([source](../sources/sn-0572.md))
* **#574 (2016-08-23)** — Exodus Intelligence bids up to $500,000 for Apple bugs; Matthew Green's team shows iMessage cryptographic weaknesses fixed in iOS 9.3. ([source](../sources/sn-0574.md))
* **#575 (2016-08-30)** — Pegasus/Trident: NSO Group's three-zero-day iOS chain found via a targeted activist and patched in iOS 9.3.5. ([source](../sources/sn-0575.md))
* **#578 (2016-09-20)** — iPhone 5c NAND mirroring bypass of passcode retry limits is demonstrated. ([source](../sources/sn-0578.md))
* **#579 (2016-09-27)** — ElcomSoft reveals iOS 10 backups can be brute-forced about 2,500 times faster; a teenager reports an iPhone 7 jailbreak within a day. ([source](../sources/sn-0579.md))
* **#581 (2016-10-11)** — Zerodium raises its iOS bounty to $1.5 million, saying full chains are harder to build. ([source](../sources/sn-0581.md))
* **#582 (2016-10-18)** — A warrant seeks to force fingerprint unlocking of all phones in a house; Leo shares the iOS 10 Home button workaround. ([source](../sources/sn-0582.md))
