# Conti ransomware dissolution

# Overview

From the Costa Rica attack to Conti's shutdown and dispersal.

# Timeline

* **#871 (2022-05-17)** — Conti crippled Costa Rican ministries, raised its ransom demand and leaked most of its stolen data. ([source](../sources/sn-0871.md))
* **#872 (2022-05-24)** — Conti's infrastructure went dark on May 19; analysts cite toxic branding, FSB pressure and sanctions, with members regrouping as other brands. ([source](../sources/sn-0872.md))
* **#874 (2022-06-07)** — Leaked Conti chats revealed research into Management Engine and UEFI persistence; Mandiant saw an Evil Corp cluster switch to LockBit to dodge sanctions. ([source](../sources/sn-0874.md))
* **#877 (2022-06-28)** — Conti's last Tor sites were shut and its crew dispersed to other groups. ([source](../sources/sn-0877.md))
