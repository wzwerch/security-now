# LastPass acquisition and security scrutiny

# Overview

LogMeIn's purchase, researcher findings and LastPass 4 features.

# Timeline

* **#529 (2015-10-13)** — LogMeIn agreed to acquire LastPass; CEO Joe Siegrist promised unchanged team and security model, while Steve and Leo stayed cautious because of LogMeIn's record. ([source](../sources/sn-0529.md))
* **#535 (2015-11-24)** — Researchers presented local-attack weaknesses in LastPass tied to convenience features; no breach, and the company fixed issues within days. ([source](../sources/sn-0535.md))
* **#541 (2016-01-05)** — LastPass 4.0 launched with emergency access through a time-delayed request; Steve promised a design review. ([source](../sources/sn-0541.md))
