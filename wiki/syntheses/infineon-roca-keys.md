# Infineon ROCA weak keys

# Overview

Factorable RSA keys from Infineon's library and national fallout.

# Timeline

* **#633 (2017-10-17)** — ROCA announced: TPM 1.2, BitLocker and some YubiKey 4 keys are factorable at modest cost. ([source](../sources/sn-0633.md))
* **#634 (2017-10-24)** — Gemalto IDPrime cards and Estonian IDs are affected; the practical risk is targeted impersonation. ([source](../sources/sn-0634.md))
* **#636 (2017-11-07)** — Bernstein and Lange improve the attack and Estonia suspends 760,000 cards. ([source](../sources/sn-0636.md))
* **#638 (2017-11-21)** — Spain's 60 million cards are called unusable and attack cost drops to about $2,000. ([source](../sources/sn-0638.md))
* **#642 (2017-12-19)** — Estonia explains its remote migration to elliptic-curve keys with timestamped signatures. ([source](../sources/sn-0642.md))
