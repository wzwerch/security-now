# Smartphone decryption bills in 2016

# Overview

State bills, Burr-Feinstein and foreign proposals.

# Timeline

* **#543 (2016-01-19)** — A New York bill to ban sale of phones the maker cannot decrypt is reintroduced. ([source](../sources/sn-0543.md))
* **#544 (2016-01-26)** — A California bill with a trafficking rationale is introduced; Steve floats physical-access escrow. ([source](../sources/sn-0544.md))
* **#545 (2016-02-02)** — Listeners point out other vendors and foreign governments would also need key vaults. ([source](../sources/sn-0545.md))
* **#551 (2016-03-15)** — Burr-Feinstein expected; the DoJ considers a WhatsApp fight. ([source](../sources/sn-0551.md))
* **#553 (2016-03-29)** — California AB-1681 would require decryptable phones from 2017 with per-device penalties. ([source](../sources/sn-0553.md))
* **#555 (2016-04-12)** — Burr-Feinstein draft text compels plaintext on court order; Hungary proposes criminalising encrypted apps. ([source](../sources/sn-0555.md))
