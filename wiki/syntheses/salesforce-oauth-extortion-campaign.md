# Salesforce OAuth data-theft campaign

# Overview

ShinyHunters, Scattered Spider and Lapsus$ collective stealing data via OAuth abuse.

# Timeline

* **#1039 (2025-08-19)** — Allianz Life's Salesforce data (about 2.8 million records) leaked after staff authorised a malicious OAuth app; the gang said ShinyHunters and Scattered Spider are the same crew. ([source](../sources/sn-1039.md))
* **#1041 (2025-09-02)** — Salesloft Drift OAuth tokens cascaded into customer systems, and TransUnion lost data on 4.4 million customers. ([source](../sources/sn-1041.md))
* **#1042 (2025-09-09)** — Scattered LapSus Hunters demanded Google fire two threat-intelligence researchers, with no evidence of a Google breach. ([source](../sources/sn-1042.md))
* **#1043 (2025-09-16)** — HackerOne confirmed Drift-related exposure, and the group posted a farewell note. ([source](../sources/sn-1043.md))
