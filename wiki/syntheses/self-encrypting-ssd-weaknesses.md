# Self-encrypting SSD weaknesses

# Overview

Research showing hardware disk encryption in popular SSDs and BitLocker's reliance on it.

# Timeline

* **#688 (2018-11-06)** — Steve teased the upcoming SSD encryption research. ([source](../sources/sn-0688.md))
* **#689 (2018-11-13)** — Seven Crucial and Samsung SSDs were broken; Microsoft advised forcing BitLocker software encryption and noted DMA key exposure risks. ([source](../sources/sn-0689.md))
* **#690 (2018-11-20)** — Listener follow-ups confirmed Windows 7 BitLocker never used hardware encryption and Samsung advised software encryption for non-portable SSDs. ([source](../sources/sn-0690.md))
