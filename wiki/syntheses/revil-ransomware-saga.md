# REvil ransomware saga

# Overview

REvil's July 2021 disappearance, brief September return, and final shutdown in October.

# Timeline

* **#828 (2021-07-20)** — All REvil infrastructure went offline at once on July 13, 2021; Steve expects a pause or rebrand and notes the Ransomwhere tracking site. ([source](../sources/sn-0828.md))
* **#829 (2021-07-27)** — Kaseya obtained a universal REvil decryptor from a third party and denied paying a ransom. ([source](../sources/sn-0829.md))
* **#836 (2021-09-14)** — REvil's leak and negotiation sites returned; a purported operator said they had taken a break, and Steve cautioned against assuming reasons. ([source](../sources/sn-0836.md))
* **#837 (2021-09-21)** — A Washington Post report said the FBI held the Kaseya decryption key for about three weeks; a DDoS campaign used the REvil name but looked like an imitator. ([source](../sources/sn-0837.md))
* **#841 (2021-10-19)** — REvil shut down for good after its new admin said its Tor setup was hijacked; a Recorded Future analyst suspected a government operation. ([source](../sources/sn-0841.md))
