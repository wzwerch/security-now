# Data brokers and privacy regulation

# Overview

Complaints, lawsuits and calls for federal law about data brokers and location data.

# Timeline

* **#689 (2018-11-13)** — Privacy International filed GDPR complaints against seven data and ad-tech firms. ([source](../sources/sn-0689.md))
* **#697 (2019-01-15)** — Los Angeles sued The Weather Channel over undisclosed location data sales. ([source](../sources/sn-0697.md))
* **#698 (2019-01-22)** — Tim Cook urged federal privacy law and a data-broker registry; Facebook ad preference controls were highlighted. ([source](../sources/sn-0698.md))
