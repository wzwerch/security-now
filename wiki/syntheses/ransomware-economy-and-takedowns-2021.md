# Ransomware economy and takedowns, 2021

# Overview

Evolution of ransomware-as-a-service, brokers and law-enforcement pressure in 2021.

# Timeline

* **#817 (2021-05-04)** — REvil extorted Quanta over Apple designs, Scripps Health was crippled, Emotet was taken down, and the Ransomware Task Force published its framework. ([source](../sources/sn-0817.md))
* **#820 (2021-05-25)** — Conti hit Ireland's HSE, CNA Financial paid $40M, and insurers began limiting ransom coverage. ([source](../sources/sn-0820.md))
* **#822 (2021-06-08)** — New victims included Fujifilm and UF Health; Steve ranted about why software security is structurally poor. ([source](../sources/sn-0822.md))
* **#824 (2021-06-22)** — Proofpoint described initial access brokers; Avaddon shut down and released keys, and AdvIntel estimated about $87M in revenue. ([source](../sources/sn-0824.md))
* **#825 (2021-06-29)** — Ireland's HSE estimated recovery at about $600M although a free decryptor was supplied. ([source](../sources/sn-0825.md))
* **#827 (2021-07-13)** — Ransomwhere began tracking payments; KELA described negotiator and broker roles. ([source](../sources/sn-0827.md))
