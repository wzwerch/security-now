# COVID-19 contact tracing apps

# Overview

Technical design, critiques and decline of exposure notification apps in 2020.

# Timeline

* **#762 (2020-04-14)** — Steve explained the Apple/Google design with daily keys, rolling identifiers and local matching, and rebutted early criticism. ([source](../sources/sn-0762.md))
* **#763 (2020-04-21)** — Listener questions about third-party apps seeing diagnosis keys; Steve preferred platform-provided apps. ([source](../sources/sn-0763.md))
* **#764 (2020-04-28)** — Eight changes to the API were announced; Steve debunked press claims about IP tracking and broadcasting of infection status. ([source](../sources/sn-0764.md))
* **#765 (2020-05-05)** — Bruce Schneier argued apps cannot work due to error rates; New York's tracing workload illustrated the scale. ([source](../sources/sn-0765.md))
* **#767 (2020-05-19)** — Utah launched its own location-based Healthy Together app, which Steve said he would use. ([source](../sources/sn-0767.md))
* **#768 (2020-05-26)** — Steve concluded voluntary apps are doomed given an estimated 80 percent adoption need and privacy fears. ([source](../sources/sn-0768.md))
