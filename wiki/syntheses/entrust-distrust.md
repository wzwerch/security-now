# Entrust distrusted by Chrome

# Overview

Entrust's compliance failures and loss of Chrome trust.

# Timeline

* **#981 (2024-07-02)** — Chrome 127 will distrust Entrust certificates issued after 31 October 2024 after repeated incidents and refusal to revoke mis-issued EV certificates. ([source](../sources/sn-0981.md))
* **#982 (2024-07-09)** — Entrust's CEO apologises; Sectigo oversells urgency while DigiCert's guidance is accurate. ([source](../sources/sn-0982.md))
* **#983 (2024-07-16)** — irs.gov is noted as using an Entrust certificate valid to 26 October. ([source](../sources/sn-0983.md))
* **#984 (2024-07-23)** — Entrust announces SSL.com as issuing partner and urges renewals before 31 October. ([source](../sources/sn-0984.md))
* **#985 (2024-07-30)** — A webinar adds that domain validation will be done by SSL.com and an Entrust intermediate will keep Entrust's name visible. ([source](../sources/sn-0985.md))
