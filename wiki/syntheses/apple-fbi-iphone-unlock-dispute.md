# Apple versus FBI over the San Bernardino iPhone

# Overview

From the All Writs Act order to the FBI buying an unlock tool.

# Timeline

* **#548 (2016-02-23)** — Steve explains the order's three demands, per-device signed updates and why policy belongs to Congress. ([source](../sources/sn-0548.md))
* **#549 (2016-03-01)** — Apple's formal reply is summarised; a New York judge refuses a similar order; strong passcodes defeat brute force. ([source](../sources/sn-0549.md))
* **#550 (2016-03-08)** — The Justice Department appeals the New York ruling. ([source](../sources/sn-0550.md))
* **#551 (2016-03-15)** — A DoJ filing hints it could demand source code and the signing key. ([source](../sources/sn-0551.md))
* **#552 (2016-03-22)** — FBI seeks a postponement after an outside party shows a possible unlock method. ([source](../sources/sn-0552.md))
* **#553 (2016-03-29)** — The government drops the case saying it accessed the data; unclear if decrypted. ([source](../sources/sn-0553.md))
* **#554 (2016-04-05)** — Schneier says the FBI method should be disclosed; the FBI offers the method to other agencies. ([source](../sources/sn-0554.md))
* **#555 (2016-04-12)** — FBI director says the tool was purchased and works only on a narrow slice of phones. ([source](../sources/sn-0555.md))
