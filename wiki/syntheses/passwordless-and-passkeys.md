# Passwordless authentication and passkeys

# Overview

Evolving passkey ecosystem, lock-in worries and vendor roadmaps.

# Timeline

* **#874 (2022-06-07)** — Steve criticizes FIDO2 passkeys for needing vendor cloud sync and lock-in, contrasting SQRL's deterministic per-site keys. ([source](../sources/sn-0874.md))
* **#875 (2022-06-14)** — Apple's WWDC session described iCloud Keychain sync and AirDrop sharing only; WebAuthn server adoption will take years. ([source](../sources/sn-0875.md))
* **#876 (2022-06-21)** — FIDO Alliance confirms bulk export is a future item; LastPass and Bitwarden plans are vague. ([source](../sources/sn-0876.md))
* **#877 (2022-06-28)** — LastPass roadmap: passwordless vault in 2022, FIDO2 vault keys later in 2022, passkey storage in 2023. ([source](../sources/sn-0877.md))
* **#883 (2022-08-09)** — A FIDO Alliance demo showed QR and Bluetooth cross-device sign-in with no cross-vendor passkey sharing. ([source](../sources/sn-0883.md))
* **#922 (2023-05-09)** — Google launches Passkeys. ([source](../sources/sn-0922.md))
* **#925 (2023-05-30)** — Bitwarden announces passkey APIs. ([source](../sources/sn-0925.md))
* **#993 (2024-09-24)** — Chrome syncs passkeys with a PIN. ([source](../sources/sn-0993.md))
* **#996 (2024-10-15)** — FIDO announces CXP. ([source](../sources/sn-0996.md))
* **#997 (2024-10-22)** — CXP mechanism analysed; Passkey Central launched. ([source](../sources/sn-0997.md))
* **#1021 (2025-04-15)** — DBSC introduced to bind sessions to hardware keys. ([source](../sources/sn-1021.md))
* **#1024 (2025-05-06)** — Microsoft makes new accounts passwordless by default and lets users delete passwords. ([source](../sources/sn-1024.md))
* **#1035 (2025-07-22)** — PoisonSeed relays cross-device passkey QR codes; on-device passkeys remain safer. ([source](../sources/sn-1035.md))
