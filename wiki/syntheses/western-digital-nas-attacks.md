# Western Digital NAS attacks

# Overview

Mass wiping of My Book Live and the unsupported My Cloud OS 3.

# Timeline

* **#825 (2021-06-29)** — My Book Live devices were factory-reset remotely; the flaw dated from 2018 and about 55,000 devices remained exposed. ([source](../sources/sn-0825.md))
* **#826 (2021-07-06)** — WD explained the 2011 authentication refactor bug, offered free recovery and trade-in, while My Cloud OS 3 remained vulnerable and unsupported. ([source](../sources/sn-0826.md))
