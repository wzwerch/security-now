# TLS 1.0 and 1.1 removal delays

# Overview

Browser plans to drop legacy TLS repeatedly postponed.

# Timeline

* **#757 (2020-03-10)** — Full-page errors for old TLS were planned for March 2020; Netcraft counted 850,000 sites still on TLS 1.0/1.1. ([source](../sources/sn-0757.md))
* **#759 (2020-03-24)** — Mozilla reversed course to keep government COVID sites reachable. ([source](../sources/sn-0759.md))
* **#761 (2020-04-07)** — Chrome moved removal to 83 and Edge to 84 or later, with legacy Edge on 8 September; SSL Labs showed over 97 percent support for modern TLS. ([source](../sources/sn-0761.md))
* **#762 (2020-04-14)** — Chrome 83 was still expected to remove TLS 1.0/1.1, though Steve doubted it would happen that year. ([source](../sources/sn-0762.md))
