# Old LinkedIn breach resurfaces and credential reuse

# Overview

The 2012 LinkedIn data sold in 2016 and the account takeovers that followed.

# Timeline

* **#561 (2016-05-24)** — 117 million email/unsalted-SHA-1 pairs from 2012 are offered for about $2,200 and most are cracked quickly. ([source](../sources/sn-0561.md))
* **#563 (2016-06-07)** — Mark Zuckerberg's reused weak password leads to his Twitter and Pinterest accounts being taken over. ([source](../sources/sn-0563.md))
* **#564 (2016-06-14)** — The seller Peace tells Wired about private resale of other breach databases for spam and targeted takeover. ([source](../sources/sn-0564.md))
* **#565 (2016-06-21)** — GoToMyPC resets passwords after a credential-reuse attack. ([source](../sources/sn-0565.md))
