# The 2019 ransomware wave

# Overview

Municipal, school, hospital and corporate ransomware throughout autumn 2019.

# Timeline

* **#728 (2019-08-20)** — Twenty-three Texas local governments hit simultaneously on August 16; an analyst calls it a game-changing first. ([source](../sources/sn-0728.md))
* **#729 (2019-08-27)** — Steve suspects a shared outsourced IT provider, with a $2.5 million demand and 22 towns affected. ([source](../sources/sn-0729.md))
* **#730 (2019-09-03)** — Armor counts 149 public incidents; schools are second-most hit; insurer negotiation at Rockville Centre and the PerCSoft dental backup attack are discussed. ([source](../sources/sn-0730.md))
* **#731 (2019-09-10)** — Flagstaff schools close; New Bedford refuses a $5.3 million Ryuk demand and restores from backups; Texas declines too. ([source](../sources/sn-0731.md))
* **#735 (2019-10-08)** — 500+ US schools, Demant's $95 million loss, a paid Ryuk decryption at an Alabama hospital system, and Victorian hospitals going manual. ([source](../sources/sn-0735.md))
* **#739 (2019-11-05)** — Armor identifies 13 MSPs and cloud providers used as ransomware distribution points in 2019. ([source](../sources/sn-0739.md))
