# Mirai, Reaper and IoT botnets

# Overview

From Mirai to the Reaper botnet and Mirai variants.

# Timeline

* **#584 (2016-11-01)** — Egress filtering and DDoS after the Dyn attack are debated. ([source](../sources/sn-0584.md))
* **#586 (2016-11-15)** — Mirai fractures into dozens of smaller botnets after its source is released. ([source](../sources/sn-0586.md))
* **#589 (2016-12-06)** — A Mirai variant disrupts UK DSL routers and Cloudflare tracks a new large botnet. ([source](../sources/sn-0589.md))
* **#599 (2017-02-14)** — A campus IoT botnet brute-forces default passwords and replaces them. ([source](../sources/sn-0599.md))
* **#634 (2017-10-24)** — IoT Reaper announced at about two million devices exploiting known flaws. ([source](../sources/sn-0634.md))
* **#635 (2017-10-31)** — F5 Labs recalibrates: 750,000 compatible devices, no attacks seen, built-in update mechanism. ([source](../sources/sn-0635.md))
* **#640 (2017-12-05)** — A Mirai variant infects about 100,000 ZyXEL routers before its domains are sinkholed. ([source](../sources/sn-0640.md))
* **#642 (2017-12-19)** — The FBI-traced origin of Mirai is revealed as students attacking Minecraft servers. ([source](../sources/sn-0642.md))
