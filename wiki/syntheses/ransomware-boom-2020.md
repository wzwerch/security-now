# Ransomware boom and new pressure tactics, 2020

# Overview

Victims, gangs and escalating extortion methods.

# Timeline

* **#786 (2020-09-29)** — One-week roundup, the UHS Ryuk attack with four reported deaths, and REvil recruiting affiliates. ([source](../sources/sn-0786.md))
* **#788 (2020-10-13)** — Carnival and Tyler Technologies breaches and Accenture research on access sellers. ([source](../sources/sn-0788.md))
* **#789 (2020-10-20)** — A Ryuk intrusion dissected step by step. ([source](../sources/sn-0789.md))
* **#792 (2020-11-10)** — Mattel, Compal, Capcom and Campari were hit and Ryuk's revenue figures were cited. ([source](../sources/sn-0792.md))
* **#793 (2020-11-17)** — Intel 471 counted 25+ RaaS portals; Ragnar Locker ran a Facebook ad. ([source](../sources/sn-0793.md))

* **#795 (2020-12-01)** — Delaware County, Canon, Ritzau and school districts were affected. ([source](../sources/sn-0795.md))
