# Tor anonymity research (2015)

# Overview

Research on traffic correlation and faster onion routing.

# Timeline


* **#484 (2014-12-02)** — Preview of research claiming over 80 percent of Tor traffic could be deanonymized. ([source](../sources/sn-0484.md))
* **#488 (2014-12-30)** — Der Spiegel NSA ranking calls Tor plus OTR catastrophic for the agency. ([source](../sources/sn-0488.md))
* **#493 (2015-02-03)** — Steve explains onion routing and why traffic correlation and NetFlow work; FBI Silk Road 2 case. ([source](../sources/sn-0493.md))
* **#494 (2015-02-10)** — Listeners propose fixes; Steve says Tor cannot be fixed because the Internet resists anonymity. ([source](../sources/sn-0494.md))
* **#514 (2015-06-30)** — Astoria: up to 58 percent of circuits expose both ends to one network; improvements cost performance. ([source](../sources/sn-0514.md))
* **#518 (2015-07-28)** — HORNET onion routing at IP layer hit about 93 Gbps in a synthetic test but is not deployable and ignores traffic analysis. ([source](../sources/sn-0518.md))
