# Russia blocks Telegram

# Overview

Court order, mass IP blocking and the end of domain fronting.

# Timeline

* **#658 (2018-04-10)** — Moscow court action seeks to blacklist Telegram after it refused to give the FSB encryption keys. ([source](../sources/sn-0658.md))
* **#659 (2018-04-17)** — Russia blocks about 15.8 million cloud IPs, breaking unrelated services, and presses app stores and VPNs. ([source](../sources/sn-0659.md))
* **#662 (2018-05-08)** — Blocks are lifted but cloud providers end domain fronting; Russia blocks 50 VPN services. ([source](../sources/sn-0662.md))
