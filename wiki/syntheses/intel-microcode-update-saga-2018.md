# Intel microcode updates 2018

# Overview

From faulty reboot-causing microcode to OS-delivered updates.

# Timeline

* **#645 (2018-01-09)** — Intel promises microcode within weeks; Steve explains that OEM BIOS pipelines will be slow. ([source](../sources/sn-0645.md))
* **#647 (2018-01-23)** — Intel withdraws its guidance over reboot problems on Broadwell, Haswell and other families; Dell offers older BIOS versions. ([source](../sources/sn-0647.md))
* **#648 (2018-01-30)** — Microsoft's out-of-band update turns off only the variant 2 mitigation. ([source](../sources/sn-0648.md))
* **#650 (2018-02-13)** — Production microcode is limited to some Skylake platforms. ([source](../sources/sn-0650.md))
* **#652 (2018-02-27)** — Kaby Lake, Coffee Lake and more Skylake reach OEMs; Windows has dormant microcode DLLs. ([source](../sources/sn-0652.md))
* **#653 (2018-03-06)** — KB4090007 delivers Intel microcode to Windows 10 1709 for two Skylake CPUs. ([source](../sources/sn-0653.md))
* **#655 (2018-03-20)** — KB4090007 now lists eight CPU IDs and March Patch Tuesday includes Skylake microcode and x86 coverage. ([source](../sources/sn-0655.md))
