# CryptoLocker ransomware saga

# Overview

From first coverage to 40 million dollars of ransom and copycats.

# Timeline

* **#428 (2013-10-30)** — Listener cases show ransom payment works; malware encrypts mapped drives; CryptoPrevent and backups discussed. ([source](../sources/sn-0428.md))
* **#429 (2013-11-06)** — Tor-based decryption service at 10 bitcoin; OpenDNS Umbrella blocks its domain flood; Sandboxie contains it. ([source](../sources/sn-0429.md))
* **#431 (2013-11-20)** — A police department paid; Bitdefender sinkhole counted about 12,000 victims in a week; 79 percent US. ([source](../sources/sn-0431.md))
* **#432 (2013-11-27)** — File format reverse-engineered, open decrypter, GRC hosts samples, ransom cut to half a bitcoin. ([source](../sources/sn-0432.md))
* **#437 (2014-01-07)** — Three successors: CryptoLocker 2, PrisonLocker and a flawed Delphi Locker. ([source](../sources/sn-0437.md))
* **#439 (2014-01-21)** — Estimated 41,928 bitcoin (over 40 million dollars) collected; internals detailed. ([source](../sources/sn-0439.md))
* **#440 (2014-01-28)** — New smaller samples evade most antivirus. ([source](../sources/sn-0440.md))
