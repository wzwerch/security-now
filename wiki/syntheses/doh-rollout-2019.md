# DNS-over-HTTPS rollout and controversy (2019)

# Overview

Browser DoH deployment, ISP resistance and Mozilla's responses.

# Timeline

* **#689 (2018-11-13)** — Cloudflare's 1.1.1.1 mobile app wrapped DNS over HTTPS in a VPN-style profile; Steve revisited DNS Benchmark and the spoofability test. ([source](../sources/sn-0689.md))
* **#690 (2018-11-20)** — Steve explained that the 1.1.1.1 certificate lists the IP in its subject alternative names. ([source](../sources/sn-0690.md))
* **#692 (2018-12-04)** — Mozilla began studying total page load time with DoH to Cloudflare. ([source](../sources/sn-0692.md))
* **#697 (2019-01-15)** — Google Public DNS added DNS over TLS and stub resolvers were noted for system-wide use. ([source](../sources/sn-0697.md))
* **#732 (2019-09-17)** — Chrome 78 beta will auto-upgrade six named DNS providers to DoH; OpenBSD and admins object to Mozilla's Cloudflare default. ([source](../sources/sn-0732.md))
* **#734 (2019-10-01)** — Mozilla details its staged US rollout with fallback, parental-control canary domain and enterprise detection; EFF weighs in. ([source](../sources/sn-0734.md))
* **#739 (2019-11-05)** — Comcast's lobbying deck against DoH is dissected and rebutted. ([source](../sources/sn-0739.md))
* **#740 (2019-11-12)** — Mozilla's letter to Congress and a survey showing all six major browsers adopting DoH. ([source](../sources/sn-0740.md))
