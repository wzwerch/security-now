# Microsoft Patch Tuesday records

# Overview

Tracking monthly record patch counts.

# Timeline

* **#771 (2020-06-16)** — 129 CVEs in June 2020, a new record. ([source](../sources/sn-0771.md))
* **#776 (2020-07-21)** — 123 fixes in July 2020. ([source](../sources/sn-0776.md))
* **#780 (2020-08-18)** — 120 fixes in August 2020, the third largest. ([source](../sources/sn-0780.md))
* **#784 (2020-09-15)** — 129 fixes in September 2020, tying the record. ([source](../sources/sn-0784.md))
* **#871 (2022-05-17)** — May 2022 update broke domain controller authentication. ([source](../sources/sn-0871.md))
* **#872 (2022-05-24)** — Microsoft shipped an out-of-band fix through the Update Catalog. ([source](../sources/sn-0872.md))
* **#880 (2022-07-19)** — July 2022 fixed 84 flaws with one exploited zero-day. ([source](../sources/sn-0880.md))
* **#881 (2022-07-26)** — July updates also duplicated USB printers and broke printing. ([source](../sources/sn-0881.md))
* **#884 (2022-08-16)** — August 2022 delivered 121 CVEs, nearly triple the prior August, including DogWalk. ([source](../sources/sn-0884.md))
* **#919 (2023-04-18)** — April 2023: 97 flaws, CLFS zero-day. ([source](../sources/sn-0919.md))
* **#928 (2023-06-20)** — June 2023: 73 flaws, 26 RCEs, no zero-days at release. ([source](../sources/sn-0928.md))
* **#1074 (2026-04-14)** — April Patch Tuesday set a record of 167 fixes. ([source](../sources/sn-1074.md))
* **#1083 (2026-06-16)** — June Patch Tuesday fixed roughly 200 Windows flaws (28 critical RCE) plus 360 Chromium/Edge fixes; Steve credits MDASH. ([source](../sources/sn-1083.md))
* **#1087 (2026-07-14)** — Microsoft's blog warns of higher update volumes; July breaks June's record. ([source](../sources/sn-1087.md))
* **#1088 (2026-07-21)** — July 2026: 570 fixes, the third record month in a row. ([source](../sources/sn-1088.md))
* **#1095 (2026-09-08)** — September 2026 Patch Tuesday: 973 fixes, 113 critical. ([source](../sources/sn-1095.md))
* **#1096 (2026-09-15)** — September 2026: record 974 fixes; 2026 passes 2,600. ([source](../sources/sn-1096.md))
* **#1097 (2026-09-22)** — The record September update causes widespread breakage despite Microsoft's three-day patching advice. ([source](../sources/sn-1097.md))
* **#1101 (2026-10-20)** — October 2026: record 1,411 fixes with few side effects; "risk cohorts" introduced. ([source](../sources/sn-1101.md))
* **#1104 (2026-11-10)** — November 2026: just under 1,100 fixes, down from October's 1,411. ([source](../sources/sn-1104.md))
