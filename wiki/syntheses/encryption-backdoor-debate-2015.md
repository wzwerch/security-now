# Overview

Congressional testimony, industry letter, FBI replies and UK plans.

* **#485 (2014-12-09)** — Judge Posner says phones the government cannot search should be illegal. ([source](../sources/sn-0485.md))

* **#490 (2015-01-13)** — David Cameron says communications the state cannot read must not be allowed. ([source](../sources/sn-0490.md))
