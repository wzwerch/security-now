# Bybit heist and Safe{Wallet} compromise

# Overview

Lazarus theft of about 1.5 billion USD and its post-mortem.

# Timeline

* **#1014 (2025-02-25)** — About 401,000 ETH stolen on 21 February 2025 by subverting the multisig signing UI; attributed to Lazarus. ([source](../sources/sn-1014.md))
* **#1015 (2025-03-04)** — Compromise traced to Safe{Wallet}; only about 42 million frozen. ([source](../sources/sn-1015.md))
* **#1016 (2025-03-11)** — Funds mostly swapped via THORChain; post-mortem says a developer ran a malicious Docker file. ([source](../sources/sn-1016.md))
