# Twitter July 2020 account takeover

# Overview

Social engineering of employees led to hijacked high-profile accounts and a bitcoin scam.

# Timeline

* **#776 (2020-07-21)** — Steve and Leo discuss the hack and reports of credentials found in an internal channel; Steve proposes multi-person approval for powerful actions. ([source](../sources/sn-0776.md))
* **#777 (2020-07-28)** — Twitter says attackers accessed DM inboxes of up to 36 of 130 targeted accounts; calls grow for end-to-end encrypted DMs. ([source](../sources/sn-0777.md))
* **#778 (2020-08-04)** — Charges announced against a 17-year-old and two others; Twitter details phone-based social engineering and Reuters reports over a thousand staff had tool access. ([source](../sources/sn-0778.md))
