# Kaseya VSA and the REvil supply-chain attack

# Overview

July 2021 REvil attack through Kaseya's remote management software.

# Timeline

* **#826 (2021-07-06)** — An authentication-bypass zero-day in Kaseya VSA was used to push ransomware via MSPs to 1,000-1,500 businesses; DIVD had already reported the bugs; demands included $70M for a universal decryptor. ([source](../sources/sn-0826.md))
* **#827 (2021-07-13)** — Because no data was stolen and backups were untouched, many victims restored instead of paying; Steve explained Sodinokibi's key hierarchy. ([source](../sources/sn-0827.md))
