# Chrome UI, sign-in and cookie controversies of 2018

# Overview

Google's unilateral browser changes and its partial reversals.

# Timeline

* **#680 (2018-09-11)** — Chrome 69 hides www and m and adds a password generator. ([source](../sources/sn-0680.md))
* **#681 (2018-09-18)** — Google reverses the www hiding temporarily, planning to hide only www in Chrome 70. ([source](../sources/sn-0681.md))
* **#682 (2018-09-25)** — Chrome 69's automatic sign-in prompts Matthew Green to quit; a flag disables it and Ungoogled Chromium appears. ([source](../sources/sn-0682.md))
* **#683 (2018-10-02)** — Chrome retaining Google cookies when clearing data causes uproar; Google promises a sign-in toggle and to clear all cookies in Chrome 70. ([source](../sources/sn-0683.md))
