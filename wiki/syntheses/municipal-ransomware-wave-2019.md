# Municipal ransomware wave 2019

# Overview

Ryuk and other ransomware against US cities, counties, courts and schools.

# Timeline

* **#720 (2019-06-25)** — Riviera Beach authorized about 600,000 USD in ransom while Atlanta and Baltimore refused and spent millions recovering. ([source](../sources/sn-0720.md))
* **#721 (2019-07-02)** — Lake City, Key Biscayne and Georgia's courts were hit; insurers paid and the Lake City IT director was fired. ([source](../sources/sn-0721.md))
* **#723 (2019-07-16)** — LaPorte County lost backups and paid about 130,000 USD; US mayors adopted an anti-payment resolution. ([source](../sources/sn-0723.md))
* **#725 (2019-07-30)** — Four Louisiana school districts triggered a state of emergency. ([source](../sources/sn-0725.md))
* **#726 (2019-08-06)** — Georgia's Department of Public Safety, Henry County and Lawrenceville were hit, forcing paper workflows; Steve urged employee training and phishing tests. ([source](../sources/sn-0726.md))
