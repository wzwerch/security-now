# Encryption and device access law (2019)

# Overview

Australia's Assistance and Access law and court rulings on forced biometric unlocking.

# Timeline

* **#693 (2018-12-11)** — Australia's bill passed the lower house; Apple warned it was dangerously ambiguous. ([source](../sources/sn-0693.md))
* **#694 (2018-12-18)** — Signal said it could not include a backdoor and may be blocked; Steve suggested OS-level hooks or per-device key escrow. ([source](../sources/sn-0694.md))
* **#697 (2019-01-15)** — A California magistrate refused to allow compelled face or finger unlocking, though courts differ. ([source](../sources/sn-0697.md))
* **#736 (2019-10-15)** — US, UK and Australia open letter urges Facebook to avoid E2EE without lawful access; Facebook declines to build backdoors. ([source](../sources/sn-0736.md))
* **#738 (2019-10-29)** — Oregon appellate ruling lets prosecutors compel a passcode under the foregone-conclusion doctrine. ([source](../sources/sn-0738.md))
* **#741 (2019-11-19)** — A Boston federal court rules suspicionless border device searches unconstitutional. ([source](../sources/sn-0741.md))
