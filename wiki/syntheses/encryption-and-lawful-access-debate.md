# Encryption and lawful access debate, late 2020

# Overview

Governments pressing for access to encrypted data in late 2015.

# Timeline

* **#529 (2015-10-13)** — Reported that the Obama administration would not seek mandatory backdoors. ([source](../sources/sn-0529.md))
* **#534 (2015-11-17)** — After Paris, media cited encryption without evidence; Steve argued bans cannot work because encryption is math. ([source](../sources/sn-0534.md))
* **#535 (2015-11-24)** — Manhattan DA proposed a statute requiring warrant-accessible smartphones; Steve found it a plausible compromise, Leo disagreed. ([source](../sources/sn-0535.md))
* **#536 (2015-12-01)** — Steve answered critics of his position; BlackBerry refused Pakistan's demand for unfettered server access. ([source](../sources/sn-0536.md))
* **#537 (2015-12-08)** — Obama asked tech leaders to make it harder for terrorists to use technology; France floated Tor and Wi-Fi bans; Kazakhstan announced a national certificate. ([source](../sources/sn-0537.md))
* **#538 (2015-12-15)** — FBI Director Comey called end-to-end encryption a business-model question; Russia pressed data localization. ([source](../sources/sn-0538.md))
* **#539 (2015-12-22)** — WhatsApp was briefly blocked in Brazil; Steve clarified per-device escrow versus a master backdoor, and Juniper served as a backdoor counterexample. ([source](../sources/sn-0539.md))
* **#617 (2017-06-20)** — UK-France, Japan, Russia and Germany announce measures against encryption and anonymity. ([source](../sources/sn-0617.md))
* **#618 (2017-06-27)** — Australia joins and will raise backdoors at the Five Eyes meeting. ([source](../sources/sn-0618.md))
* **#620 (2017-07-18)** — Australia's PM says its laws override mathematics; Steve muses about warranted interception via a CA-capable certificate. ([source](../sources/sn-0620.md))
* **#621 (2017-07-25)** — IETF debate on static Diffie-Hellman in TLS 1.3 for data-center decryption. ([source](../sources/sn-0621.md))
* **#624 (2017-08-15)** — UK data protection bill and calls for global treaties. ([source](../sources/sn-0624.md))
* **#788 (2020-10-13)** — Five Eyes plus Japan and India renewed calls for access. ([source](../sources/sn-0788.md))
* **#792 (2020-11-10)** — EU Council draft resolution; Steve said strong encryption cannot be uncreated. ([source](../sources/sn-0792.md))
* **#794 (2020-11-24)** — Android Messages adopted the Signal protocol for RCS. ([source](../sources/sn-0794.md))
