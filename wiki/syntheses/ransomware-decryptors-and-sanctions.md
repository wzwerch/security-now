# Ransomware decryptors and payments

# Overview

Cases where ransomware could be decrypted free or where payments were traced.

# Timeline

* **#688 (2018-11-06)** — CommonRansom asked victims for remote desktop access to decrypt. ([source](../sources/sn-0688.md))
* **#692 (2018-12-04)** — US authorities sanctioned SamSam cash-out addresses holding about 23 million dollars. ([source](../sources/sn-0692.md))
* **#694 (2018-12-18)** — Decryptors were available for weak families such as InsaneCrypt and Hidden Tear derivatives. ([source](../sources/sn-0694.md))
* **#696 (2019-01-08)** — Bleeping Computer joined No More Ransom. ([source](../sources/sn-0696.md))
* **#697 (2019-01-15)** — A PyLocky decryptor required captured network traffic. ([source](../sources/sn-0697.md))
