# IoT security failures 2017

# Overview

A running catalog of insecure connected devices.

# Timeline

* **#584 (2016-11-01)** — A boot-time bug in the EdgeRouter X makes it act as a switch briefly after power-up; Ubiquiti published a fix script. ([source](../sources/sn-0584.md))
* **#585 (2016-11-08)** — Steve suggests IoT may only be safe via hubs run by Apple or Google, citing a fridge that lost calendar sync. ([source](../sources/sn-0585.md))
* **#586 (2016-11-15)** — Researchers take over Philips Hue bulbs by ZigBee from far away using a shared signing key. ([source](../sources/sn-0586.md))
* **#587 (2016-11-22)** — Low-cost Android phones with Adups and Ragentek firmware; Schneier tells Congress everything is now a computer; a camera is infected in 98 seconds. ([source](../sources/sn-0587.md))
* **#589 (2016-12-06)** — Sony IP cameras with hidden accounts, a Mirai variant taking down UK routers, and a USB-killer device. ([source](../sources/sn-0589.md))
* **#598 (2017-02-07)** — About 150,000 printers exposed through UPnP; Vizio TV viewing data settlement. ([source](../sources/sn-0598.md))
* **#599 (2017-02-14)** — A university's thousands of IoT devices are infected and flood DNS; lessons include segmenting IoT networks. ([source](../sources/sn-0599.md))
* **#601 (2017-02-28)** — CloudPets exposed 800,000 credentials and two million recordings, and the toys lacked Bluetooth pairing. ([source](../sources/sn-0601.md))
* **#604 (2017-03-21)** — An app-connected adult toy maker settled a $3.75 million class action over undisclosed usage data collection. ([source](../sources/sn-0604.md))
* **#605 (2017-03-28)** — Nest cameras had Bluetooth DoS bugs left unpatched for four months and a commercial dishwasher had directory traversal. ([source](../sources/sn-0605.md))
* **#606 (2017-04-04)** — Smart TVs were shown vulnerable to malicious broadcast signals; Z-Wave introduced S2 security. ([source](../sources/sn-0606.md))
* **#607 (2017-04-11)** — Brickerbot destroyed devices; Garadget disabled a customer's door opener after a bad review. ([source](../sources/sn-0607.md))
* **#609 (2017-04-25)** — Ten flaws in 25 Linksys router models and Bose app listening-history lawsuit. ([source](../sources/sn-0609.md))
