# EU eIDAS Article 45 and browser trust

# Overview

The EU plan to force browsers to trust member-state certificates and the community response.

# Timeline

* **#947 (2023-11-07)** — Main topic: Article 45 would force browsers to trust government-designated certificates; open letter with 466 signatories and a Mozilla-led second letter. ([source](../sources/sn-0947.md))
* **#948 (2023-11-14)** — Steve explains root stores and interception; no new developments beyond press coverage. ([source](../sources/sn-0948.md))
* **#949 (2023-11-21)** — A listener argues for QWACs as super-EV; Steve rebuts. A reader counted 165 Firefox roots. ([source](../sources/sn-0949.md))
* **#950 (2023-11-28)** — Firefox 120 imports OS roots (aimed at enterprises); listeners ask about removing EU roots. ([source](../sources/sn-0950.md))
* **#951 (2023-12-05)** — Provisional agreement analyzed via Ivan Ristic and Ryan Hurst: 43 new roots, limits on distrust, seven CAs cover 99 percent of certificates. ([source](../sources/sn-0951.md))
* **#952 (2023-12-12)** — Idea of a UI colour extension is dismissed in favour of trusting only about six CAs. ([source](../sources/sn-0952.md))
* **#953 (2023-12-19)** — Chrome Root Program launch (September 2023) means Chromium no longer uses OS roots. ([source](../sources/sn-0953.md))
* **#955 (2024-01-02)** — Community tool reports which roots a browser actually used; manual removal via the Windows MMC snap-in. ([source](../sources/sn-0955.md))
* **#956 (2024-01-09)** — Untrusting by dragging roots to the Untrusted Certificates store is reversible. ([source](../sources/sn-0956.md))
