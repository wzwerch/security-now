# PewDiePie fan hacks

# Overview

A fan hacker abused exposed printers and Chromecasts to push YouTube subscriptions.

# Timeline

* **#692 (2018-12-04)** — A hacker printed promotional pages on exposed printers using the PRET toolkit, and a printer-spam service appeared. ([source](../sources/sn-0692.md))
* **#694 (2018-12-18)** — A second blast hit about 100,000 printers and a newspaper site was defaced. ([source](../sources/sn-0694.md))
* **#696 (2019-01-08)** — CastHack hijacked Chromecast, TV and Google Home devices at scale. ([source](../sources/sn-0696.md))
