# Clearview AI legal troubles

# Overview

Regulatory actions against the face-scraping database.

# Timeline

* **#752 (2020-02-04)** — Clearview is sued under Illinois BIPA after a New York Times exposé; Facebook settles a separate BIPA case for 550 million dollars. ([source](../sources/sn-0752.md))
* **#753 (2020-02-11)** — Twitter, Facebook, Google and YouTube send cease-and-desists while the hiQ v. LinkedIn ruling frames the scraping law. ([source](../sources/sn-0753.md))
* **#756 (2020-03-03)** — A breach exposes Clearview's customer list across law enforcement and private companies. ([source](../sources/sn-0756.md))
* **#872 (2022-05-24)** — Illinois settlement, UK fine and use in Ukraine for identifying the dead. ([source](../sources/sn-0872.md))
* **#880 (2022-07-19)** — Greece fined Clearview 20M euros under GDPR; France and Italy ruled similarly and Austria was expected to follow. ([source](../sources/sn-0880.md))
