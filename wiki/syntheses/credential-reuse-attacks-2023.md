# Credential reuse attacks in 2023

# Overview

Norton LifeLock, PayPal and Chick-fil-A account takeovers.

# Timeline

* **#906 (2023-01-17)** — About 6,450 Norton LifeLock accounts compromised via credential stuffing. ([source](../sources/sn-0906.md))
* **#907 (2023-01-24)** — PayPal reports almost 35,000 accounts hit; Steve explains the technique and recommends TOTP plus device cookies. ([source](../sources/sn-0907.md))
* **#913 (2023-03-07)** — Chick-fil-A loses 71,473 accounts; Steve traces it to stolen Facebook credentials and the risk of single sign-on. ([source](../sources/sn-0913.md))
