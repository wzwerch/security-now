# Late 2018 breaches and disclosure

# Overview

Marriott, Quora, Google+, Facebook and database exposures and the debate over disclosure.

# Timeline

* **#692 (2018-12-04)** — Marriott disclosed up to 500 million Starwood guest records; Quora lost about 100 million accounts' data; an open Elasticsearch exposed 57 million. ([source](../sources/sn-0692.md))
* **#693 (2018-12-11)** — Google found and fixed a Google+ API bug itself, and 12 states sued a health records vendor over breach handling. ([source](../sources/sn-0693.md))
* **#694 (2018-12-18)** — A shareholder suit against Google and Facebook's photo API disclosure raised concerns that punishing disclosure will discourage it. ([source](../sources/sn-0694.md))
