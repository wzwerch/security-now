# Google FLoC in 2021

# Overview

Rise, pushback and delay of FLoC cohort tracking.

# Timeline

* **#814 (2021-04-13)** — EFF's amifloced.org test appeared; FLoC works from a SimHash of a week of browsing. ([source](../sources/sn-0814.md))
* **#815 (2021-04-20)** — Vivaldi and others disabled FLoC; Google's paper described t-closeness protections against homogeneity attacks. ([source](../sources/sn-0815.md))
* **#816 (2021-04-27)** — A listener's cohort ID suggested a limited number of cohorts; Microsoft's competing proposal was mentioned. ([source](../sources/sn-0816.md))
* **#819 (2021-05-18)** — A listener's cohort ID changed within weeks. ([source](../sources/sn-0819.md))
* **#825 (2021-06-29)** — Google delayed broader rollout toward 2023 amid EU and UK regulatory scrutiny and no other browser adopting it. ([source](../sources/sn-0825.md))
