# SIM swap authentication weaknesses

# Overview

Weak carrier and site authentication enabling phone number hijacks.

# Timeline

* **#742 (2019-11-26)** — Twitter lets users drop SMS 2FA after its CEO was hijacked via a SIM swap and SMS tweeting. ([source](../sources/sn-0742.md))
* **#746 (2019-12-23)** — SIM swapping is listed as emerging in 2015 in the decade review. ([source](../sources/sn-0746.md))
* **#749 (2020-01-14)** — Princeton shows five US prepaid carriers swap SIMs with weak challenges, and 17 sites fall to a swap alone. ([source](../sources/sn-0749.md))
