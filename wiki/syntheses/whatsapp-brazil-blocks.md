# WhatsApp blocks and fines in Brazil

# Overview

Repeated Brazilian court actions against WhatsApp for not producing encrypted messages.

# Timeline

* **#558 (2016-05-03)** — A judge orders a three-day WhatsApp shutdown which is overturned within a day. ([source](../sources/sn-0558.md))
* **#567 (2016-07-05)** — Facebook funds are frozen to cover accumulated fines. ([source](../sources/sn-0567.md))
* **#569 (2016-07-19)** — A judge again suspends WhatsApp until compliance, which is impossible by design. ([source](../sources/sn-0569.md))
