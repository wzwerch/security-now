# SandboxEscaper Windows zero-days

# Overview

Researcher repeatedly dropped Windows privilege escalation zero-days with public proofs of concept.

# Timeline

* **#687 (2018-10-30)** — A Windows 10 Data Sharing Service flaw demo deleted a boot-critical file; 0patch offered a micropatch and Microsoft said it qualified for bounty. ([source](../sources/sn-0687.md))
* **#689 (2018-11-13)** — Steve grouped her releases with other full disclosures such as the VirtualBox escape. ([source](../sources/sn-0689.md))
* **#696 (2019-01-08)** — A fourth zero-day in the Windows Installer arrived before her social accounts and repositories were removed. ([source](../sources/sn-0696.md))
* **#697 (2019-01-15)** — Zerodium's raised Windows privilege escalation payouts underscored what she forwent. ([source](../sources/sn-0697.md))
* **#716 (2019-05-28)** — Five new exploits appeared after a public outburst; one was already fixed and the rest were local privilege escalations. ([source](../sources/sn-0716.md))
* **#718 (2019-06-11)** — A second bypass of the CVE-2019-0841 fix appeared; Steve considered it a crude hack that 0patch could not reproduce. ([source](../sources/sn-0718.md))
* **#719 (2019-06-18)** — June Patch Tuesday fixed four of the earlier exploits. ([source](../sources/sn-0719.md))
