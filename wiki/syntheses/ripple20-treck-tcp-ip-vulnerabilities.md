# Ripple20 Treck TCP/IP vulnerabilities

# Overview

19 flaws in an embedded TCP/IP stack used in hundreds of millions of devices.

# Timeline

* **#772 (2020-06-23)** — JSOF disclosed Ripple20: 19 vulnerabilities in Treck's TCP/IP stack, four critical, exploitable via valid-looking packets, with most devices unlikely ever to be patched. ([source](../sources/sn-0772.md))
* **#773 (2020-06-30)** — Follow-up lists affected vendors and Forescout counts; healthcare devices were most exposed and about 15,000 internet-reachable devices were identifiable. ([source](../sources/sn-0773.md))
