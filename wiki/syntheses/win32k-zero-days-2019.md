# win32k.sys zero-days in 2019

# Overview

Series of exploited Windows kernel privilege escalations.

# Timeline

* **#705 (2019-03-12)** — A Windows 7 win32k flaw was paired with a Chrome bug. ([source](../sources/sn-0705.md))
* **#706 (2019-03-19)** — March patches fixed it and a second variant used by FruityArmor and SandCat. ([source](../sources/sn-0706.md))
* **#710 (2019-04-16)** — April patches fixed two more; Kaspersky had reported seven in six months. ([source](../sources/sn-0710.md))
* **#711 (2019-04-23)** — The April flaw saw heavy exploitation after patching. ([source](../sources/sn-0711.md))
* **#713 (2019-05-07)** — Kaspersky tied a March win32k bug to the seller Volodya. ([source](../sources/sn-0713.md))
