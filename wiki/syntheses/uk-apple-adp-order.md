# Overview

UK secret demand for access to ADP data and the global reaction.

# Timeline

* **#1013 (2025-02-18)** — US lawmakers Wyden and Biggs push back; Wyden circulates a CLOUD Act amendment. Steve objects to the word backdoor. ([source](../sources/sn-1013.md))
* **#1014 (2025-02-25)** — Apple stops offering ADP to new UK users on 21 February 2025. Listeners choose 'master key' as replacement for 'backdoor'. ([source](../sources/sn-1014.md))
* **#1015 (2025-03-04)** — Listener note that disabling ADP uploads keys to Apple. Signal threatens to leave Sweden over similar access law. ([source](../sources/sn-1015.md))
* **#1016 (2025-03-11)** — Apple appeals to the Investigatory Powers Tribunal; Home Office will not confirm the order exists. ([source](../sources/sn-1016.md))
* **#1017 (2025-03-18)** — Google declined to deny receiving a technical capability notice; groups ask for Apple's hearing to be public. ([source](../sources/sn-1017.md))
