# Shadow Brokers leaks to WannaCry

# Overview

NSA-linked exploit leaks that became DoublePulsar infections and the WannaCry worm.

# Timeline

* **#608 (2017-04-18)** — The Shadow Brokers dumped more NSA tools (EternalBlue and others); Microsoft said all were fixed, most in March. ([source](../sources/sn-0608.md))
* **#609 (2017-04-25)** — Scans found tens of thousands of machines already carrying DoublePulsar and over five million exposing port 445. ([source](../sources/sn-0609.md))
* **#612 (2017-05-16)** — WannaCry hit the NHS and others using EternalBlue; Steve explained the worm, its kill-switch domain and blame for unpatched XP. ([source](../sources/sn-0612.md))
* **#613 (2017-05-23)** — Aftermath: NSA disclosure debate, PATCH Act, key recovery with WanaKiwi, variant growth and about 417,000 infected IPs. ([source](../sources/sn-0613.md))
