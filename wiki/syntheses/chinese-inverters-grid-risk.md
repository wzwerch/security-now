# Undocumented radios in Chinese inverters

# Overview

Reuters reporting on rogue communication hardware and remote shutdown risk in solar inverters and batteries.

# Timeline

* **#1026 (2025-05-20)** — Reuters reported undocumented cellular radios in some Chinese inverters and batteries and a November 2024 remote shutdown; responses included a Senate bill, utility actions and a Lithuanian law. ([source](../sources/sn-1026.md))
* **#1027 (2025-05-27)** — Listeners discussed hidden kill-switch triggers via radio or satellite broadcast and how losing a grid slice can cascade, as with the Iberian blackout; the cause of that outage was not established. ([source](../sources/sn-1027.md))
