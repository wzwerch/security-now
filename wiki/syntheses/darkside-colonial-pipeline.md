# DarkSide and the Colonial Pipeline attack

# Overview

From the May 2021 shutdown to DarkSide's closure and bitcoin seizure.

# Timeline

* **#818 (2021-05-11)** — Colonial Pipeline shut down on May 7 after DarkSide ransomware; the FBI confirmed attribution and states declared emergencies. ([source](../sources/sn-0818.md))
* **#819 (2021-05-18)** — Mandiant and Elliptic analysis detailed the RaaS structure and the 75 BTC payment; DarkSide announced it was closing and forums banned RaaS. ([source](../sources/sn-0819.md))
* **#820 (2021-05-25)** — Steve predicted ransomware escrow services after affiliates complained about unpaid commissions. ([source](../sources/sn-0820.md))
* **#821 (2021-06-01)** — Colonial reportedly paid $4.4M but restored mostly from backups because the DarkSide decryptor was too slow. ([source](../sources/sn-0821.md))
* **#822 (2021-06-08)** — Mandiant evidence pointed to a compromised, MFA-less VPN account; the DOJ seized 63.7 BTC, which Steve suspects came from political pressure on DarkSide. ([source](../sources/sn-0822.md))
