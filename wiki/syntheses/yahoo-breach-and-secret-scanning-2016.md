# Yahoo breach and secret email scanning

# Overview

From ignored state-actor warnings to a half-billion-account breach and government-ordered mail scanning.

# Timeline

* **#570 (2016-07-26)** — A DNC consultant ignored daily Yahoo warnings of state-sponsored attacks on her personal account. ([source](../sources/sn-0570.md))
* **#579 (2016-09-27)** — Yahoo discloses a 500-million-account breach known for two years; Steve discusses bcrypt, partial hash upgrades and advises changing reused passwords. ([source](../sources/sn-0579.md))
* **#581 (2016-10-11)** — Reuters reports secret 2015 scanning of all incoming mail for intelligence; Yahoo also disables new mail forwarding; Verizon seeks a $1 billion discount. ([source](../sources/sn-0581.md))
* **#582 (2016-10-18)** — LastPass starts warning about Yahoo passwords; Leo notes Yahoo recycles inactive addresses. ([source](../sources/sn-0582.md))
