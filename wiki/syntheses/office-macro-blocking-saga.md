# Office macro blocking saga

# Overview

Microsoft's back-and-forth on blocking internet VBA macros and attacker adaptation.

# Timeline

* **#879 (2022-07-12)** — Microsoft rolled back its default macro block as temporary after business feedback, then reversed the rollback; Steve retells macro virus history from Concept onward. ([source](../sources/sn-0879.md))
* **#881 (2022-07-26)** — Microsoft confirmed resumption of the block in Current Channel and updated admin docs; Steve dismisses finance-sector objections and favors signed macros. ([source](../sources/sn-0881.md))
* **#882 (2022-08-02)** — Proofpoint data showed macro attachments down about two thirds; attackers moved to ISO, RAR and LNK containers that evade Mark of the Web. ([source](../sources/sn-0882.md))
