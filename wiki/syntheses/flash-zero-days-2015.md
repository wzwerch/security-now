# Adobe Flash zero-days and decline (2015)

# Overview

Series of Flash exploits and platform moves against the plugin.

# Timeline

* **#514 (2015-06-30)** — APT3 exploited an FLV-parsing zero-day using ROP; fixed in 18.0.0.194. ([source](../sources/sn-0514.md))
* **#516 (2015-07-14)** — A second Hacking Team Flash zero-day fixed in 18.0.0.209 and used to deliver ransomware. ([source](../sources/sn-0516.md))
* **#520 (2015-08-11)** — Flash 18.0.0.232 released; Steve urges running plugins only on demand. ([source](../sources/sn-0520.md))
* **#523 (2015-09-01)** — Amazon banned Flash ads and Chrome changed its plugin default. ([source](../sources/sn-0523.md))
* **#526 (2015-09-22)** — Another critical update (19.0.0.185); Forbes malvertising used seven Flash exploits in one file. ([source](../sources/sn-0526.md))
