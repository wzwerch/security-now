# EU Chat Control

# Overview

Repeated EU attempts to mandate scanning of private communications.

# Timeline

* **#871 (2022-05-17)** — EU proposal would require detection of known and new CSAM and grooming even in encrypted chats; Steve calls it technically unworkable mass surveillance. ([source](../sources/sn-0871.md))
* **#877 (2022-06-28)** — Germany's digital minister said the proposal crosses a line while the commissioner defended it. ([source](../sources/sn-0877.md))
* **#1036 (2025-07-29)** — Denmark's EU presidency reintroduces Chat Control for an October 14 debate; Steve prefers on-device AI to backdoors. ([source](../sources/sn-1036.md))
* **#1046 (2025-10-07)** — Vote set for October 14 with 12 states in favor and 8 against; Signal threatens to leave the EU; the Netherlands says no and Steve argues the OS is the right place for any filtering. ([source](../sources/sn-1046.md))
* **#1047 (2025-10-14)** — Vote cancelled after Germany and the Netherlands opposed it; 40 European firms sent an open letter citing digital sovereignty. ([source](../sources/sn-1047.md))
* **#1070 (2026-03-17)** — Parliament extends only the voluntary scanning exception to August 2027 with limits; no permanent mandate. ([source](../sources/sn-1070.md))
* **#1086 (2026-07-07)** — A further trilogue/vote looms; Signal expected to leave the EU if it passes. ([source](../sources/sn-1086.md))
