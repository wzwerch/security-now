# Black October and Internet-scale AI-found flaws

# Overview

From October 2026, AI-found flaws with Internet-wide reach, starting with PHP2Shell, forced embargoed fixes, emergency patching and temporary disconnections; Cloudflare's Matthew Prince warned more were already known.

# Timeline

* **#1102 (2026-10-27)** — PHP2Shell hits PHP 5.4 onward; leaks, Chainguard's early images and exploitation follow; eMudhra's tiny CA keys add to "Black October". ([source](../sources/sn-1102.md))
* **#1103 (2026-11-03)** — AI-found Linux MPTCP race and a leaked EDK II PXE overflow follow PHP2Shell. ([source](../sources/sn-1103.md))
* **#1104 (2026-11-10)** — AI-found SSHole in OpenSSH on musl is exploited within 40 hours; an AI-enabled hacker dumps 30+ N-central zero-days. ([source](../sources/sn-1104.md))
