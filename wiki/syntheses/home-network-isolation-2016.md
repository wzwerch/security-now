# Home and IoT network isolation (2016)

# Overview

From three dumb routers to the EdgeRouter X and pfSense.

# Timeline

* **#570 (2016-07-26)** — Listeners praise the EdgeRouter X; Steve warns that VLAN tags are not isolation and that a plain router switch has no separation. ([source](../sources/sn-0570.md))
* **#571 (2016-08-02)** — Steve gives a tutorial on per-port subnets, ordered rules and stateful filtering for IoT isolation. ([source](../sources/sn-0571.md))
* **#573 (2016-08-16)** — PC Perspective publishes a three-dumb-routers write-up; Steve notes blocking ports 137-139 and 445 and restricting UPnP in pfSense. ([source](../sources/sn-0573.md))
* **#574 (2016-08-23)** — Steve finds cheap consumer routers contain a Qualcomm switch chip with advanced hidden features; DD-WRT can reach some of them. ([source](../sources/sn-0574.md))
* **#575 (2016-08-30)** — Another dumb-router guide and an EdgeRouter YouTube channel are added to GRC's Link Farm. ([source](../sources/sn-0575.md))
* **#577 (2016-09-13)** — Brian asks how to tell an EdgeRouter X from a managed switch; Steve says listings rarely make it clear. ([source](../sources/sn-0577.md))
