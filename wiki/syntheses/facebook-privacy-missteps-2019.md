# Facebook privacy missteps in 2019

# Overview

Series of Facebook data-handling problems and regulatory fallout.

# Timeline

* **#700 (2019-02-05)** — Facebook paid users to install a research app requiring its root certificate, via Apple's enterprise programme. ([source](../sources/sn-0700.md))
* **#709 (2019-04-09)** — Facebook showed an interstitial asking for email passwords. ([source](../sources/sn-0709.md))
* **#711 (2019-04-23)** — It admitted harvesting 1.5 million contact lists and more plaintext Instagram passwords. ([source](../sources/sn-0711.md))
* **#712 (2019-04-30)** — A $3 to $5 billion FTC provision was disclosed to shareholders. ([source](../sources/sn-0712.md))
