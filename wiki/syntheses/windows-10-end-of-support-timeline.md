# Windows 10 end of support and ESU

# Overview

ESU terms, Office support extension and criticism of Windows 11 requirements.

# Timeline

* **#857 (2022-02-08)** — Steve pre-announces a tool to stay on Windows 10 after concluding Windows 11 hardware limits are arbitrary. ([source](../sources/sn-0857.md))
* **#858 (2022-02-15)** — InControl released. ([source](../sources/sn-0858.md))
* **#860 (2022-03-01)** — Windows Weekly agrees TPM 2.0 requirement was arbitrary; Windows 10 support ends October 2025. ([source](../sources/sn-0860.md))
* **#866 (2022-04-12)** — Microsoft announces Windows Autopatch; 20H2 and 1909 reach end of service in May 2022. ([source](../sources/sn-0866.md))
* **#867 (2022-04-19)** — Steve contrasts router auto-update with Autopatch as capitulation on Windows quality. ([source](../sources/sn-0867.md))
* **#1026 (2025-05-20)** — Microsoft extended Office app support on Windows 10 to October 2028 though OS support ends 14 October 2025. ([source](../sources/sn-1026.md))
* **#1032 (2025-07-01)** — A first ESU year was offered free with Windows Backup or 1,000 Rewards points, otherwise 30 dollars. ([source](../sources/sn-1032.md))
* **#1036 (2025-07-29)** — ESU enrollment starts appearing; Steve plans to stay on Windows 10 and expects 0patch coverage. ([source](../sources/sn-1036.md))
* **#1038 (2025-08-12)** — GRC's InControl was found to block KB5001716, the update that nags about end of support. ([source](../sources/sn-1038.md))
* **#1044 (2025-09-23)** — Consumer Reports urged extending free updates; about 46% of Windows users remained on Windows 10. ([source](../sources/sn-1044.md))
* **#1045 (2025-09-30)** — Manual trick to force the ESU offer; EEA users get free ESU with no conditions. ([source](../sources/sn-1045.md))
* **#1047 (2025-10-14)** — October 14, 2025 is the last Patch Tuesday for Windows 10 outside ESU; IE Mode exploit led Microsoft to hide it further. ([source](../sources/sn-1047.md))
* **#1085 (2026-06-30)** — ESU extended again to October 2027; Steve predicts a 'Junior 11'. ([source](../sources/sn-1085.md))
